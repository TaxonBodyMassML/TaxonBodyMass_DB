# Provenance rounds (issue #1)

source and round, in the order they were run (the template of issue #1,
section 4). Read by no code; the machine-readable state is in
`sources/databases/<Src>/primary_references.csv`, `Bib/pending_citations.csv`,
`Bib/scite_checks.csv` and `reports/warnings_citations.md`.

## 2026-10-04 -- Herberstein_etal_2022 (pilot A0, Stage 1 of issue #1; hop 2)

Tooling `tbmcite 0.1.0` (branch `issue-1-pilot-herberstein`, merged with main f1eee70 after the Hebert pilot).

- Parser: `BodyMass_Herberstein_etal_2022.r` keeps the hash key `h:<sha1-8>` of the normalised `fullReference` as `ref_keys` (`ParseInRowCitations()`; 2,856 records, 0 NA, 193 keys; one in-text form, "Terblanche, Klok, & Chown, 2004", stands for two papers, which is why the full text and not the in-text form is hashed). The in-row parser gained `labels`: the record's `inTextReference` becomes the reference's `note` (`in-text: ...`) so that the hash keys stay readable (`reflist_specs` `intext_col`).
- Hop 2 (issue #1, 1.3) implemented in `BuildProvenance()`: a resolved reference whose CiteID, bib key or DOI is a registered source label is an intermediate compilation (`MatchIntermediateLabel()`); the intermediate's own resolved references for the species give `compiled_via_compilation` rows at hop 2 with `via_cite_id` (`IntermediateReferences()`), a `self` reference of the intermediate gives `compiled_from` citing it, and otherwise the intermediate is the terminal citation (`compilation_terminal`, owner decision). First case: Chown et al. 2007 (244 records, 8 ant species, Chown's own Appendix S1 measurements) -> 7 accepted species `compilation_terminal` at `Chown_etal_2007` until Chown is ingested. `Chown:2007aa` received `doi` and `volume` in the curated bib and the source `Citation.bib` (owner-accepted hand edit) so that the DOI route and the key reuse of `--bib` recognise it.

OpenAlex now limits keyless use to 1,000 requests a day and answers the rest with 429 and a day-long Retry-After, for which httr2 slept ("Waiting 83003s for retry backoff"): `CachedGET()` treats such a 429 as an exhausted quota (classed condition `citations_quota`, host flagged for the session), `VerifyPrimaryReferences()` leaves the affected references unverified and reports them, `OPENALEX_API_KEY` is sent when set (never stored: the cache key and the stored URL drop it). ## 2026-10-04 -- Oskyrko_2024 (ReptTraits; tier A2, issue #77; Phases A and B, no PR)

Tooling `tbmcite 0.1.0` (branch `issue-77-oskyrko-2024` from main edcffcf).

- Source: ReptTraits v1.2 (Oskyrko, Mi, Meiri & Du 2024, Scientific Data 11:243; figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.24572683, CC BY 4.0), the four sheets as csv. Trait T20 `Maximum body mass (g)` holds 10,469 species maxima; the paper's Methods say "most mass data are based on lengths, transformed to masses based on taxon-specific equations", and the values confirm it: 5,957 are identical to the Feldman_etal_2016 or Meiri_2018 SVL-derived value of the species and 8,937 (85%) are within 0.0105 log10 of it. The sources sheet cites the length's source, not a weighed animal.
- Parser: `BodyMass_Oskyrko_2024.r` drops, through `DropImputed()`, 9,765 rows cited to the length-mass allometry compilations (Slavenko et al. 2019, Zimin et al. 2022, Meiri et al. 2021 -- "we converted lengths to masses using the allometric equations published by Feldman et al.", maxima -- and the Feldman works), 619 rows whose value is identical to the Feldman_etal_2016 / Meiri_2018 value of the species and 29 within 0.0105 of it; 56 records remain (40 squamates, 15 turtles, 1 crocodilian), none demonstrably measured (owner decision pending, see the README). The citation cell of every kept row is `ref_keys` (`SplitRefKeys(adat$mass_src, ',')`; 56 of 56 keyed, 22 keys). Registry: `Oskyrko_2024 -> Feldman_etal_2016` (copies, 0.001) and `Oskyrko_2024 -> Meiri_2018` (derived_same_input, 0.001), documenting the removed copies; at accepted-name level the kept frame still shares 25 species with Feldman_etal_2016, 16 identical (Feldman values under synonyms, collapsed by the edge: `reports/dedupe_summary.md`).
- Pipeline (recompile, downloads off): 38,566 -> 38,581 species (+17 new, -2 removed by the range filter because of an Oskyrko value: Philodryas boliviana 1,245.6 vs 66.3 g Feldman; Chironius quadricarinatus, under its ReptTraits synonym Phrynonax sexcarinatus, 681.3 vs 61 g Makarieva), 10,725 -> 10,728 genera; 52 species rows carry the label (Zonateres lanei unresolved by every enrichment stage; Alligator mississippiensis already removed by the range filter before this source).
- Reference list: `references.csv` (`key`, `citation`) transcribed from `ReptTraits dataset v1-2_references.csv` for the 22 keys of the kept records: 19 verbatim entries (three matched by the species' family where the list holds two entries of the author and year: Kraus 2017a, Shine et al. 2006 -> 2006a, Wallach & Gemel 2018), 3 keys absent from the list (Aresco 2009; Wilson and Heinsohn 2007; Wilson et al. 2006b).

Container vol(issue), pages, (year).") parsed to an empty title (or to a page number, "1770-74") and every reference scored `below_threshold` although Crossref's first candidate was the right work. A bracketed year that closes the entry is now the year, and the author block ends at the first title word (`SplitAuthorsFromTitle()`, `normalise_citation.r`; five new parse tests). The first `--init --verify --queue` pass (19 `below_threshold` rows) was discarded before anything was committed: `Bib/pending_citations.csv` reverted to HEAD, `primary_references.csv` and the report deleted, and the pass run once more with the fixed parser.
