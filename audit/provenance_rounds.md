source and round, in the order they were run (the template of issue #1,
section 4). Read by no code; the machine-readable state is in
`sources/databases/<Src>/primary_references.csv`, `Bib/pending_citations.csv`,
`Bib/scite_checks.csv` and `reports/warnings_citations.md`.
