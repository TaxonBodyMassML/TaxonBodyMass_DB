# StoichLife (González et al. 2025): body dry masses of the animals analysed for elemental content

Source: González, A. L., Merder, J., Andraczek, K., Brose, U., Filipiak, M., ... & Dézerald, O. (2025) StoichLife: A global dataset of plant and animal elemental content. Scientific Data 12:569. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04852-w (open access). Bib key `Gonzalez:2025aa` (`Citation.bib`); source label `Gonzalez_2024` (the owner's label for issue #74; the article and the Dryad deposit are dated 2025).

## Data

Dryad 10.5061/dryad.3tx95x6r2 (version 15, published 2025-03-01, licence CC0 1.0 per the Dryad record; the Dryad README does not state a licence itself), downloaded by M. Novak 2026-10-04 as the workbook `datapaper_2025_02_11.xlsx` (6.6 MB) and exported sheet by sheet: `datapaper_2025_02_11_data.csv` (sheet Data, 28,049 records x 38 columns, 10.5 MB), `datapaper_2025_02_11_metadata.csv` (sheet Metadata Descriptions: variable, description, type, percent NA, range) and `datapaper_2025_02_11_references.csv` (sheet References: `data.source1`, `data.type`, `reference`, `DOI`; 89 rows). `README_dryad.md` is Dryad's own README (`README.md` in the deposit). The compilation holds 19,664 animal and 8,385 plant records (`kingdom_revised`), one row per analysed individual or pooled sample, with %C, %N, %P and their ratios, coordinates, habitat, the taxonomy as found and as revised by the compilers (`*_init`, `*_revised`, `species.level` y/n) and, for 9,942 animal rows, `body.weight.gr`, documented as "body dry mass (g)" (Metadata). The data came from 24 contributors' templates (50 datasets, `data.type` template), 33 papers of a Web of Science search (`paper`) and six existing databases (`Vanni_database`, `Ikeda_database`, `Elser_database`, and three plant databases). There is no sex, life-stage or sample-size column; a `template` row's `data.source1` is `unpublished - <contributor>` when the data were not published before StoichLife.

Columns used: `sp.morphosp_revised` (the binomial), `species.level` (filter), `body.weight.gr` (dry mass, g), `kingdom_revised`, `phylum_revised`, `class_revised`, `order_revised`, `family_revised` (filter, conversion groups and taxonomy hints), `habitat` (conversion groups and the immature-insect filter), `data.source1` (kept as `ref_keys`), `data.type` (reference list only). The elemental columns, coordinates and environmental covariates are not used.

## Record set

Filters: animal rows (`kingdom_revised == 'Animalia'`) with a positive `body.weight.gr` (9,942), of which only the rows identified to species (`species.level == 'y'`: 4,541 rows, 361 binomials, all of the form `Genus species` after the compilers' revision) are used; the 5,401 rows of morphospecies (`Formicidae sp.11`, `Chironomidae sp.2`, `Culex sp.`, `Copepod nauplii copepodids`) and common-name labels (`Orange mite`) leave with `species.level == 'n'`. No fossil taxa. The exclusions below remove 965 further rows. 3,576 records of 336 species from 50 `data.source1` keys remain: 1,795 ray-finned fishes (28 species, 19 keys), 1,296 arthropods (573 insects, 412 malacostracans, 119 copepods, 118 spiders, 49 centipedes, 20 millipedes, 5 ostracods), 140 molluscs, 87 reptiles, 50 mammals (one species, *Lepus americanus*), 48 chaetognaths, 32 hydrozoans, 55 helminths (trematodes, acanthocephalans, nematodes), 52 annelids and 14 birds. One row per individual (`n = 1`), so the species value is the geometric mean over individuals of Pass 1 in RunMe.r; the individuals are those a field stoichiometry study sampled, so a species value here is a field mean of whatever sizes were analysed, not a handbook adult mass (the fishes in particular are smaller than the database's FishBase values; see the hand-back of issue #74 for the per-source comparison).

Mass type: body DRY mass in grams (`body.weight.gr`, Metadata), converted to wet grams with `ToWetMass(from = 'dry')` and the group factors of `R/library/mass_conversion.r`, the group chosen from the revised class or phylum and the habitat: `insect` (Studier & Sevick 1992; 0.35) for Insecta, Arachnida, Chilopoda, Diplopoda (and Collembola, Diplura, Symphyla, none at species level; the non-insect terrestrial arthropods by analogy, as `Hishi_etal_2019`), `fish` (Brey 2010) for Actinopterygii, `vertebrate` (the generic 0.25 of Peters 1983, no conversion CiteID, so these records keep the bare label) for Reptilia, Aves and Mammalia, `crustacean_zooplankton` (Kiørboe 2013) for Copepoda, Ostracoda and the marine Malacostraca (the pelagic euphausiids, hyperiid amphipods, mysids and decapods of the Ikeda database), `invertebrate` (Brey 2010) for the freshwater Malacostraca (atyid shrimps, *Xiphocaris*, a crayfish), `mollusc` for Mollusca, `annelid`, `chaetognath`, `gelatinous_zooplankton` (Kiørboe 2013, Lucas 2011) for Hydrozoa and Thaliacea and `helminth` (Brey 2010 by analogy) for Platyhelminthes, Nematoda and Acanthocephala. The dry mass of bivalves (freshwater unionids of Christian et al. 2008, *Mytilus* of Jansen et al. 2012) and of the freshwater snail *Tarebia granifera* is the soft tissue analysed for stoichiometry and is scaled to whole wet mass including the shell with `whole = TRUE` and the Brey 2010 shell ratios (`bivalve`, `gastropod`); the pelagic pteropods of the Ikeda database (*Limacina*, *Clio*, the naked *Clione*) are treated as whole animals, as in `Ikeda_2014`. Check of the factors against the database (species x source geometric means vs `TaxonBodyMass.csv`, 107 pairs): median log10(database / converted) within 0.2 for insects, copepods, malacostracans, chaetognaths, birds, mammals and reptiles; fishes 0.74 (the database's FishBase values are maximum weights and several sources sampled juveniles); spiders 0.40 and centipedes 0.98 (few pairs, small individuals). Values with `source_mass` `Gonzalez_2024; Brey_2010` (2,254), `Gonzalez_2024; Studier_1992` (760), `Gonzalez_2024; Kiorboe_2013` (372), `Gonzalez_2024; Kiorboe_2013; Lucas_2011` (39) and bare `Gonzalez_2024` (151, the generic vertebrate factor).

Imputed rows: none flagged in source (StoichLife compiles measured individuals and carries no imputation flag). Four exclusions are made through `DropImputed()` (logged to `audit/imputed_rows.csv`), in this order: (1) 183 rows (8 species) of freshwater Odonata and Trichoptera (nymphs and larvae by biology; Ephemeroptera, Plecoptera and Megaloptera would be treated alike but have no species-level rows) and the freshwater Diptera of González et al. (2018), the mosquito larvae of bromeliad tanks (*Culex*, *Anopheles*); the nine freshwater Diptera of Hambäck et al. (2009) are adults classified by larval habitat and are kept; (2) 212 rows of `unpublished - Roussel`: *Salmo trutta* and *Salvelinus fontinalis* at 0.18-0.32 mg "dry mass", below the dry mass of a salmonid egg, so the unit cannot be grams (unverifiable units); (3) 36 rows of `unpublished - Harrower`: ant workers at 0.4-6.7 ug "dry mass" (*Camponotus vicinus* 6.7 ug, *Solenopsis molesta* 0.4 ug, two to three orders below published worker masses) beside *Tapinoma sessile* at 0.4 mg (units inconsistent within the dataset); (4) 534 rows (7 species) of Amphibia: the stream-salamander rows (Milanovich 2007, 2008, Milanovich & Hopton 2014, Milanovich & Maerz) are larvae and the anuran rows (Rugenski 2013, Moody et al. 2017, Vanni et al. 2002) tadpoles and metamorphs (*Rhinella marina* at 3 mg dry, *Desmognathus quadramaculatus* at 0.05-0.1 g dry); all 13 species x source values sit 1.5-4 log10 below the database's adult values, so the class is dropped as non-adult records. The file has no life-stage column, so no other stage filter is possible; the fishes of the Vanni database sources (McIntyre et al. 2008, Torres & Vanni 2007, Small et al. 2011, `unpublished - Paseka`) include small individuals of large species (*Lepomis macrochirus* at 2.4 g wet, *Hoplias malabaricus* at 13 g) and are kept pending the owner's decision (issue #74). 3,576 rows kept.

Source label: `Gonzalez_2024` (with the conversion CiteIDs above).

## Provenance

Provenance: compilation (`Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`: class `compilation`, default type `unknown`); record-level, hop 1 through the `data.source1` key of every row (an author-year key, an `unpublished - <contributor>` template label, or the sub-source of a database; one key per row), kept as `ref_keys` (`SplitRefKeys()`; the one key naming two papers, `Jochum et al. 2016 / Drescher et al. 2016`, is split into both, and is cited by no species-level row). Reference list `references.csv` (columns `key`, `citation`, `doi`, `note`), built by `build_references.r` from the Dryad sheet References (`datapaper_2025_02_11_references.csv`, kept verbatim): the 33 `paper` and 50 `template` rows keep their key, citation and DOI (every DOI of the sheet is kept as given, including the bioRxiv DOI of Rizzuto et al. 2019 and the bioRxiv DOI the sheet gives Goncharov et al. 2016, which belongs to Rizzuto); the six database rows (`data.source1` `-`) are keyed by their `data.type`, and the records' key `Ikeda database` is mapped to the `Ikeda_database` row (Ikeda & Skjoldal 1989); the 22 sub-sources of the Vanni database (`Vanni et al 2002`, `McIntyre et al 2008`, `Milanovitch_2007`, `Vanni, MJ unpubl`, ...) and `Urabe 1993` of the Elser database are not in the sheet, so their key is kept as the citation text with the database reference (Vanni et al. 2017 Ecology 98:1475, 10.1002/ecy.1792; Elser et al. 2000 Nature 408:578) in `note`; the `unpublished - <contributor>` template rows cite the StoichLife article itself in the sheet, and the key is prepended to that citation so that the tooling reads them as unpublished data of the compilers (role `self`, provenance `measured_in_source`: first published in StoichLife); `unpublished - Jackson` and `unpublished - Potapov` (one row with a trailing blank) are listed twice in the sheet and once here; `unpublished - Rosanova` is cited by the records but absent from the sheet (which has `unpublished - Rosanova / Tiunov`) and is added as a template row with a note. 112 keys, 50 cited by the kept records (1,425 records from 19 `paper` keys, 854 from 17 `template` keys, 450 from the Ikeda database, 847 from 13 Vanni-database sub-sources), 62 cited by no kept record (plant sources, morphospecies-only datasets, the amphibian and excluded sources). Overlaps with other sources of the database: the Ikeda database rows (36 pelagic species) are Ikeda's individual measurements, whose species means `Ikeda_2014` compiles; `Tande 1982` (*Calanus finmarchicus*) and Ikeda & Skjoldal 1989 are also references of `Kiorboe_2013` and `Hebert_etal_2016`; Jankowski (2000) is a lab-Sheet label (`Jankowski_2000`, *Craspedacusta sowerbii*), whose Sheet value overrides the compiled records (`Bib/source_dependencies.csv`, hand-back of issue #74). Verification: round of 2026-10-04 (`tbmcite 0.1.0`), see `audit/provenance_rounds.md` and `primary_references.csv`.
