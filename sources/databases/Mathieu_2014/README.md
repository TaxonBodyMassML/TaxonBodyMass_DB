# French earthworm traits (Mathieu & Davies 2014, after Bouche 1972)

Source: Mathieu, J., & Davies, T. J. (2014) Glaciation as an historical filter of below-ground biodiversity. Journal of Biogeography 41:1204-1214. https://doi.org/10.1111/jbi.12284
Data: `traits.txt` (100 species, traits compiled from Bouche, M. B. (1972) Lombriciens de France: ecologie et systematique, INRA), with `sites&species.txt` and its README; downloaded by M. Novak 2026-09-29.

Columns used: `species.name` (Fauna Europaea names, '.' separated), `maximal_weight` (mg), `family`.
Filters: species with a weight (97). Values are maximum fresh body masses (e.g. Lumbricus terrestris 15,000 mg).
Mass type: fresh mass, mg -> g.
