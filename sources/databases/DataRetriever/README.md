# rdataretriever sources (five Ecological Archives data papers)

Source: five data papers fetched live through the Python `retriever` package (rdataretriever) by `R/library/data_retrieve.r` when `DataRetrieve = TRUE` in `R/RunMe.r`, and saved together as `sources/Rdata/BodyMass_DataRetrieverAll.Rdata`. No raw files are stored in this folder.
Data: retriever datasets `mammal-life-hist`, `predator-prey-body-ratio`, `pantheria`, `amniote-life-hist` and `socean-diet-data`, downloaded at each run (`bird-size`, Lislevand et al. 2007, was fetched here until 2026-10-05, issue #102: the same file is parsed by `sources/databases/Lislevand_etal_2007/`, and the retriever copy -- male mass only, no reference numbers -- duplicated it under the same label) (see the main README, Prerequisites, for the retriever venv).
Licence: unknown; owner to check https://retriever.readthedocs.io (nothing is stored here: the five Ecological Archives data papers are fetched at run time by rdataretriever and their terms are stated in each data paper's Ecological Archives metadata, which is not on disk; the tracked csv files of this folder are our own unit decisions and reference tables, under the repository licence). See sources/LICENSES.md, "Not redistributed".

## Brose_2005 (issue #14, owner decisions 2026-10-03)

Brose et al. (2005) compiled consumer and resource body sizes of 16,866 trophic links from 30 studies (`link_reference`), with `mean_mass_g` per side (the mean mass of the individuals involved in the link, a population mean that the database uses as reported, owner decision 2026-10-03, issue #29; for fish such means sit below the adult masses of other sources) and a free-text `body_size_methodology`; the paper does not state whether masses are fresh or dry, and the file's `notes` say "Dry body masses are species-specific regressions" for the Broadstone Stream study only. Six of the studies were later incorporated into GATEWAy (`Brose_etal_2018`), partly revised (registry edge in `Bib/source_dependencies.csv`). `R/library/data_retrieve.r` handles the table with the helpers of `R/library/foodweb_units.r`:

1. Life stage: consumer and resource rows are stacked and only adult (any value starting with 'adult'), female, male or unspecified (`-999`) life stages are kept, the pattern of the GATEWAy parser (`StackBrose2005()`, `AdultOrUnspecified()`); juveniles, larvae, immatures, nymphs, pupae and nauplii are dropped (before #14 every stage entered). Rows without a taxonomy entry are dropped (the Yodzis 1998 functional groups, the Warren 1989 lengths-only rows). 19,175 stacked rows remain of 26,112.
2. Group-level placeholder values (a mean mass carried by five or more species-level names of one study) are dropped as imputed (`DropPlaceholders()`): 2,269 rows of 68 names, all in the Grand Caricaie study.
3. Per-study actions from `brose2005_units.csv` (`ApplyUnitActions()`; column `action`): `convert_dry` for Woodward & Hildrew (Broadstone Stream; dry mass stated in the file's notes; 4 rows and 2 species remain after the life-stage filter), Ledger, Edwards & Woodward (Mill Stream; dry status inferred from the Woodward group's conventions and from the values, 0.51x the GATEWAy copy of the same web and 1.5 log10 below the other sources; 98 rows, 10 species) and Cattin Blandenier 2004 (Grand Caricaie; invertebrates only: species by species they are 0.36x the GATEWAy copy of the same web, median log10 -0.44, IQR 0.15, n = 188, with the insects at the insect dry:wet ratio, while the 12 vertebrates are identical to GATEWAy's fresh masses, so Brose 2005 holds Cattin's dry masses and GATEWAy the converted ones; this replaces the `keep` of the Phase A table and is flagged in PR #14); `keep` for every other study (Tuesday Lake is identical to its GATEWAy copy; Weddell Sea, Celtic Sea, Mulgrave River, nematodes, parasitoids, Australian consumers as reported). Conversion groups per taxon come from `brose2005_units_groups.csv` (389 taxa; `MassGroupFromRanks()` on the enrichment phylum and class: insect 0.35, invertebrate 0.2144, mollusc 0.1972 as whole wet mass through the Brey 2010 shell ratios for shelled taxa (slugs and sea slugs take the tissue factor only), annelid 0.1769, fish 0.2415; `convert = FALSE` for the Grand Caricaie vertebrates), and `LabelWithConversion()` appends the conversion CiteIDs. 13,616 rows are converted (5,647 insects with Studier & Sevick 1992, 7,969 other taxa with Brey 2010 factors); 16,906 rows enter the per-taxon geometric mean.

Provenance (Brose_2005): compilation (`Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`: class `compilation`, default type `unknown`), attributed at **study level** like GATEWAy (owner decision 2026-10-04, issue #64): every row's primary reference is the study of its `link_reference`, whose authors measured or compiled the masses of their web (the masses are "published here for the first time" for most studies, per the Ecological Archives metadata), and `data_retrieve.r` keeps `ref_keys` = the key(s) of the row's study through `WebReferenceKeys()` and `brose2005_references.csv` (columns `key`, `link_reference`, `citation`, `doi`, `note`, `owner_review`; one row per cited work, first author's surname plus year, `Ruess_unpublished` for an unpublished contribution, `HarperSmith_inpress` for a chapter in press; the compound 'Woodward & Hildrew (2001); Woodward et al., in press.' gives `Woodward_2001; Woodward_2005`; the page-range variants of the Warren 1989 reference are one study, `StackBrose2005()`), kept per record (Pass 1 of `RunMe.r` collapses them per species with `JoinRefKeys()`). `citation` is the verbatim entry of the metadata's Literature cited (Ecological Archives E086-135, `metadata.htm` on figshare record 3525119, a free file read, not stored) or, for the unpublished contributions, the phrase of the metadata's Introduction; the `Body size reference` column (11 values, mostly the same studies, Andrassy 1956 for the nematodes) is not used. 20 studies give 21 keys; 12 are cited by the frame's 16,905 Brose_2005 records, 475 taxa after the #14 filters (taxa per key: Cattin Blandenier 246, Jonsson 50, Pinnegar 45, Ledger 37, Rayner 28, Ruess 27, Jacob 24, Ulrich 2001 8, Ulrich 1999 5, Dell 5, Woodward 2; records per key in `primary_references_Brose_2005.csv`, column `n_records`), the other 9 studies reach the pipeline with no row (functional groups or lengths only). The frame that built main until 2026-10-05 (md5 `1443be65...`) predated the keys, and `recompile = TRUE` does not rebuild a live frame; the frame was regenerated under #69 (Regeneration below), so the keys now reach `TaxonBodyMass_Provenance.csv.gz`. Round of 2026-10-04 (`tbmcite 0.1.0`, on the regenerated frame): `primary_references_Brose_2005.csv` (the per-label file of this shared folder) holds 12 rows: 1 certain (Pinnegar et al. 2003, two-service agreement), 4 approved by the owner (Jonsson et al. 2005 for the 'Jonsson et al. (2004)' study, Woodward et al. 2005, Woodward & Hildrew 2002 for 'Woodward & Hildrew (2001)', the Cattin Blandenier 2004 thesis), 2 DOI-less entries from the metadata fields (Ulrich 1999, Ulrich 2001), 5 rejected: the unpublished contributions of Ledger, Edwards & Woodward (Mill Stream); Ruess (nematodes); Rayner (Mulgrave River); Jacob, Brey & Mintenbeck (Weddell Sea); Dell (Australia) -- owner decision 2026-10-04: unpublished contributions are not cited as primary references, the compilation is the citable source (the contributors' names stay here and in the `notes` of both reference files). 355 of 475 taxa (74.7%) cite an accepted reference; since the regeneration of 2026-10-05 the Brose_2005 rows of `TaxonBodyMass_Provenance.csv.gz` carry these statuses (466 rows: 196 approved, 17 certain, 13 nodoi_approved, 69 rejected, 171 conversion_factor; `reports/warnings_citations.md`: 94.0% of 8,941 record links resolved, before: 0% `unknown`). `--init --verify` re-run on the regenerated frame on 2026-10-05: 16,905 records keyed (0% NA), 12 native keys, statuses unchanged (certain 1, approved 4, nodoi_approved 2, rejected 5), only `n_records` rewritten from taxa to records. Bib entries in `Bib/TaxonBodyMass_PrimaryCitations.bib` (7 keys, 2 shared with Brose_etal_2018), 5 new rows appended to the Sheet tab `BM_primary_citations`. Log: `audit/provenance_rounds.md`.

Tables: `brose2005_units.csv` (one row per study: `n_rows`, `n_species`, `n_shared`, `median_log10_ratio`, `iqr_log10_ratio` to the other sources after the life-stage filter, `unit_class`, `proposed_action`, `factor`, `evidence`, `refs`, `action`, `mass_group`, `log_reason`), `brose2005_units_decisions.csv` (hand-made, one row per study) and `brose2005_units_groups.csv` are regenerated by `Rscript sources/databases/DataRetriever/brose2005_units_analysis.r` from the repository root, which reads the retriever's csv of the table (written to `tmp/retriever/` by the venv python when absent, or `BROSE2005_CSV`), needs `sources/Rdata/` and `sources/enrich_cache.Rdata`, and writes `tmp/brose2005_units_species.csv` with the study x species detail.

## Provenance of Myhrvold_2015 (Amniote; issue #1, Stage 2, round of 2026-10-06)

Compilation (registry: class `compilation`, default type `unknown`); record-level, hop 1 through the per-cell source names of `Amniote_Database_References_Aug_2015.csv` (the dataset's second table, `references`, in the same EAV layout as the data; joined by the retriever's `record_id`, the row of the data file), kept as `ref_keys` by `data_retrieve.r` since the frame of 2026-10-06 and resolved in `sources/databases/Myhrvold_2015/references.csv` (`key`, `reference_name`, `citation`, `note`, `owner_review`; 105 rows, written by `build_references.py` from the archive zip in the retriever cache) for the citation tooling (`reflist_specs$Myhrvold_2015`, frame `DataRetrieverAll`, folder `Myhrvold_2015`, `review_col = 'owner_review'`, `compilation_doi` 10.1890/15-0846R.1).

- Cell grammar (`AmnioteValueSources()`, `R/library/foodweb_units.r`; `test_retriever_ref_keys.R`): an `adult_body_mass_g` cell reads `<name>` (10,689 records), `<name> from median of n(<name>, ...)` (the stored value is the median of the n raw values in the brackets and equals the value <name> reported) or `mean of <name> & <name> from median of n(...)` (5,988 records; an even n, the mean of the two middle values, '&' or 'and'). The names before "from median of" are the sources of the value and become its keys (12,510 records with one key, 4,167 with two); the names in the brackets (232 distinct) contributed to the median but the value is not theirs and they are not keys. A name may carry the prefix `BC Birds - ` / `BC mammals - ` / `BC Reptiles - ` (505 occurrences; a batch mark of the compilers' data entry, not part of the reference: the same name occurs with and without it), which is dropped. One cell writes an empty name (`mean of  & Iverson, ...`). A name is one token of the dataset's own vocabulary (Supplemental Table 2, 1,003 names with their record counts, the two with a diacritic matched by their ASCII spelling of the csv), which is what lets a cell be split although a name itself holds ', ' and ' and '.
