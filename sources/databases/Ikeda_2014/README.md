# Ikeda (2014) marine metazooplankton dry masses

Source: Ikeda, T. (2014) Respiration and ammonia excretion by marine metazooplankton taxa: synthesis toward a global-bathymetric model. Marine Biology 161:2753-2766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00227-014-2540-5
Data: Springer Electronic Supplementary Material `227_2014_2540_MOESM1_ESM.pdf` (tables S1 respiration, S2 ammonia excretion, S3 O:N ratio, S4 data codes -> species/stage), downloaded by M. Novak 2026-09-28.

Parsing: `parse_ikeda_esm.py` runs `pdftotext -layout`, splits each table's side-by-side column groups at the header positions, reads them in column order carrying the taxon-group label (COPE, EUPH, AMPH, DECA, MYSI, OSTR, CNID, CTEN, THAL, APPE, CHAE, MOLL, POLY) and species down through blank cells, and resolves 'see S4' codes through table S4. Each table's title ('S3. Summary of O:N ratio data') and column-header line precede its data rows and are excluded by position (the parser reads only the lines below the header line); every remaining non-blank column piece is classified and counted on stderr as parsed, skipped malformed, title/header or footnote. Output `ikeda2014_esm_parsed.csv`: 1,160 records (S1 671, S2 267, S3 222) from 1,191 pieces. 28 malformed pieces are skipped and each is reported (S1 16, S2 2, S3 10): codes '14S'/'14L'/'24S'/'24L', a decimal depth (MOLL 11c), 'see  S4' with two spaces, rows without a species name (S1 CHAE S4, S9-S13, THAL S15; S3 CHAE S3, S8, THAL S11, APPE S2-S3), 'misc doliolids', an 'outlier!' tail, a species glued to its depth ('Pegea confoederata15'), a truncated 'Tomopteris carpenteri' line and two codes missing from S4 (S1 CNID 39, S3 MOLL 4); 3 pieces are fragments of the footnote 'Italic codes/data are outliers and did not included in the analyses' (one per table); no title or header piece occurs. Until #11 a filter meant for table titles also dropped, uncounted, 65 S3 pieces whose code (S1, S2, S3, S10, ...) starts the piece because the row carries no group label, 59 of them records (S3 COPE S2-S3, S10-S39; DECA S1-S3; OSTR S2-S3; CHAE S2, S10-S12; THAL S2-S3, S10, S12-S14, S16-S25; POLY S2-S3); the parser now keeps them, so the CSV is a complete transcription of S1-S3 (the old 1,101 records are unchanged). 'CHNI' in S3 is treated as a typo of CNID. The tables' Reference column (author-year key of the primary study) is kept as the last CSV column `ref`: the ESM prints the key only on the first record of each run of records from one study, so the parser fills it down in reading order, column by column within a table (e.g. COPE 231-253 at the top of the second column of S1 inherit 'Ikeda et al. (2007)' from COPE 1), and also consumes keys and group labels printed on lines that it does not parse as records (e.g. the malformed S1 THAL S15, S3 CHAE S3, CHAE S8, THAL S11 and POLY S1 lines; S3 prints each group label once, so the two records after S3 POLY S1 are labelled POLY only because of this) so that the fill-down stays in step with the PDF. A key is accepted only if it matches a strict author + '(year)' / '(unpublished data)' pattern ('outlier!' and footnote fragments found in the reference position are ignored and reported on stderr), and four typographic variants are canonicalised: 'Ikeda et al. ( 2001)' -> 'Ikeda et al. (2001)', 'Lombard et al .(2005)' -> 'Lombard et al. (2005)', 'Kaeriyama & Ikeda (2004)' -> 'Kaeriyama and Ikeda (2004)', 'Ikeda unpublished data' -> 'Ikeda (unpublished data)'. Every parsed record carries a key (107 explicit: S1 44, S2 35, S3 28; 5 more consumed from malformed lines: S1 1, S3 4; the rest filled down). Known limit of the S3 transcription (immaterial for the frame, which excludes S3): the ESM credits S3 DECA S1-S9 to 'Quetin et al.1980', printed without parentheses, which the strict pattern rejects (reported on stderr), so these nine rows inherit 'Ikeda (2013d)'.

Columns used: species, stage, taxon_group, dw_mg (individual dry mass, mg), ref (reference key of the primary study, carried as `ref_keys`).
Filters: copepodite stages C1-C5, juveniles (J) and stage text embedded in the species field (e.g. 'Calanoides acutus C4,5') are dropped; C6 adults, F/M/FG/A, salp aggregate/solitary forms and unstaged records are kept. Genus-level and 'misc' names dropped; subspecies/forms truncated to binomials. Table S3 (O:N ratio; 222 parsed records, 196 after these filters) is then excluded in full with `DropImputed()`, because its rows are the O:N measurements of the same specimens as Tables S1 (respiration) and S2 (ammonia excretion) and would only raise `n` for those taxa without new information (owner decision, #11).
Mass type: dry mass, mg -> g, converted with group factors in R/library/mass_conversion.r: crustacean groups -> crustacean_zooplankton (dry = 0.20 x wet), CNID/CTEN/THAL/APPE -> gelatinous_zooplankton (dry = 0.045 x wet), CHAE -> chaetognath (dry = 0.0785 x wet), MOLL -> mollusc (0.1972), POLY -> annelid (0.1769), all species-level medians from the Brey et al. 2010 Conversion04 data bank.
Source labels: 'Ikeda_2014; Kiorboe_2013' (crustaceans), 'Ikeda_2014; Kiorboe_2013; Lucas_2011' (gelatinous groups), 'Ikeda_2014; Brey_2010' (CHAE/MOLL/POLY).
Provenance: compilation (registry `Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`: class `compilation`, default type `unknown`). The ESM's Reference column is kept as `ref_keys` through `SplitRefKeys(adat$ref, ';')` (one key per record, so the ';' separator never occurs), in the tables' own author-year form: 'Ikeda (2013a)', 'Ikeda and Mitchell (1982)', 'Ikeda et al. (2007)', 'Ikeda (unpublished data)'; 792 of 792 kept records are keyed, 37 distinct keys (1,160 parsed, 988 after the stage/name filters, 792 after the S3 exclusion). The ESM has no reference list of its own, so the reference list is the one the paper's Crossref record deposits (`reflist_specs` format `crossref_reflist`, `compilation_doi` 10.1007/s00227-014-2540-5: 101 references, 89 with a DOI; `ReflistFromCrossrefReferences()` joins each key to the one deposited reference with the same first surname, diacritics folded, year, suffix and author form -- one author, the two surnames, three or more for 'et al.'; all 36 published keys join uniquely, 34 with a deposited DOI, Ikeda 1974 and Roger 1988 without; the deposited `unstructured` text is `raw_citation`, the Crossref reference key is kept in `notes`). `primary_references.csv` (round of 2026-10-05, tbmcite 0.1.0): 33 references certain through the deposited DOI (`doi_resolves`: Crossref and OpenAlex both resolve the DOI and agree on first author and year; title similarity 0.49-1.00, the low values being Mayzaud & Dallot 1973, whose title the parser cuts at 'I.', and Biggs 1977, 'ammonia' vs 'ammonium'); 1 `self` ('Ikeda (unpublished data)', 3 records); 1 pending for the owner (Bamstedt 1979, a book chapter in Naylor & Hartnoll eds., Pergamon, 2 records; the deposited DOI 10.1016/B978-0-08-023217-1.50041-2 resolves at both services and is the only candidate); 2 not found (Ikeda 1974, Mem. Fac. Fish. Hokkaido Univ. 22: 1-97, 19 records, the reference the owner deferred in the Kiorboe_2013 round; Roger 1988, Oceanol. Acta 11: 107-116, 2 records; neither indexed by Crossref or OpenAlex). Owner decisions of 2026-10-05 in `Bib/pending_citations.csv`, not yet applied: Bamstedt 1979 `1`, Ikeda 1974 and Roger 1988 `nodoi` (DOI-less entries from the parsed deposited citations). Records resolved to a primary reference: 769 of 792 (97.1%); in `TaxonBodyMass_Provenance.csv.gz` the label has 683 rows of 297 species (339 `compiled_from` hop 1, 331 of them certain, 1 `measured_in_source`, 343 `conversion_factor`), without CiteIDs until `--bib`; six of the 33 DOIs already have entries in `Bib/TaxonBodyMass_PrimaryCitations.bib` from the Kiorboe_2013, Hebert_etal_2016 and Gonzalez_2025 rounds (`Ikeda:1982aa`, `Ikeda:1986aa`, `Ikeda:1989aa`, `Ikeda:2001aa`, `Iguchi:2004aa`, `Gorsky:1987aa`), which `--bib` reuses by DOI.
