# Guo & Bailly (2024) weights of preserved marine fishes in the UBC Beaty Biodiversity Museum Fish Collection

Source: Guo, Z., & Bailly, N. (2024) Length-weight relationships of marine fishes using preserved specimens: a contribution of the UBC Beaty Biodiversity Museum Fish Collection. In D. Pauly & V. Ruiz-Leotaud (Eds.), Marine and Freshwater Miscellanea V (pp. 23-42). Fisheries Centre Research Reports 32(4). Institute for the Oceans and Fisheries, University of British Columbia, Vancouver. ISSN 2816-4083; open access; no DOI exists for the chapter or for FCRR 32(4) (Crossref and DataCite checked 2026-10-02; the volume also has no cIRcle handle); FishBase RefNo 132372. Volume PDF: https://fisheries-2023.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2024/09/Marine-and-Freshwater-Miscellanea-V-Spet20.pdf (the chapter's own 'Cite as' footnote is on p. 23). Bib key `Guo:2024aa` (`Citation.bib`).
Data: `GuoBailly_2024_AppendixTable1.csv`, a transcription of the chapter's Appendix Table 1 (pp. 29-35: 'Total (TL) and standard length (SL) of 303 preserved species at Beaty Biodiversity Museum of UBC, B.C., Vancouver, with the parameter a of length-weight relationships set at b = 3'): 303 rows, columns `Species`, `TL_cm`, `SL_cm`, `Weight_g`, `a_TL`, `a_SL`. In the repository since 2026-08-20 (from FracFeed_DB, as `Pauly_2024_AppendixTable1.csv`). Checked against the PDF with pdftotext on 2026-10-03: all 303 names and all TL, SL and weight values match the printed table. Appendix Table 2 (pp. 36-42; family, species with authority and BBM catalogue number of each of the 303 specimens) is not transcribed. The printed Table 1 carries the source's own autocorrect artefacts, kept verbatim in the CSV and handled downstream: nine epithets turned into English words or spellings, seven of them corrected by rules in `R/library/fix_misspellings.r` (lines 337-468: `Anotopterus pharaoh` -> pharao, `Brosmophycis marginate` -> marginata, `Crystallodytes cookie` -> cookei, `Enophrys taurine` -> taurina, `Pholis ornate` -> ornata, `Pterois lunulate` -> lunulata, `Tilesina gibbose` -> gibbosa) and two (`Muraena lentiginose` -> lentiginosa, `Chirolophis decorates` -> decoratus) resolved by GBIF fuzzy matching during enrichment without a rule; and seven capitalised epithets (`Gymnothorax Saxicola`, `Sebastes Saxicola`, `Bathygobius Andrei`, `Lythrypnus Rhizophora`, `Gillellus Arenicola`, `Cantherhines Rapanui`, `Guentheridia Formosa`) normalised by `FixFormatting()`. Appendix Table 2 prints the correct spelling in every case.
Licence: transcription (facts) (README, Citation.bib: open-access chapter, no DOI, no CC licence stated). `GuoBailly_2024_AppendixTable1.csv` is our transcription of Appendix Table 1 and is attributed to the chapter; the chapter is not stored.

Columns used: `Species`, `Weight_g`. `TL_cm`, `SL_cm`, `a_TL` and `a_SL` are not used.
Filters: none; all 303 rows are used (the script would drop a row without a numeric `Weight_g`, but none occurs).
Mass type: wet mass in grams (weighed to 0.1 g on a top-loading balance) of one preserved museum specimen per species; no conversion. The specimens are formalin-fixed, stored in 45% isopropanol and were gently dried with a paper towel before weighing; for each species the authors chose the best-preserved specimen and, among suitable ones, the largest (Methods, p. 24). Caveats: preservation shrinks both length and weight relative to fresh fish (the chapter cites a 15% weight loss in formalin within 10 days; p. 26), so the values are biased low; and each value is a single specimen, not a species mean (`n = 1` per species).
Source label: `GuoBailly_2024`. The label was `Pauly_2024` (after the volume editors, bib key `Pauly:2014aa`) from 2026-08-20 until 2026-10 (issue #3); files under `audit/` written before then still use the old label. The parse script deletes a leftover `sources/Rdata/BodyMass_Pauly_2024.Rdata` (git-ignored, so a pull does not remove it) when it finds one, so an existing checkout migrates at its next recompile instead of stopping at RunMe's stale-frame check.
Imputed rows: none flagged in source.
Provenance: primary, measured_in_source (hop 0): the authors measured and weighed the BBM specimens themselves; catalogue numbers in Appendix Table 2.
