# Chown et al. (2007) insect metabolic rate compilation

Source: Chown, S. L., Marais, E., Terblanche, J. S., Klok, C. J., Lighton, J. R. B., & Blackburn, T. M. (2007) Scaling of insect metabolic rate is inconsistent with the nutrient supply network model. Functional Ecology 21:282-290. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2435.2007.01245.x
Data: Wiley Supporting Information `fec1245_supmat.pdf` (36 pages; the publisher's Supporting Information file for the article: Appendix S1, pages 1-5, metabolic rate data for eight size-polymorphic ant species, 244 individual records; Appendix S2, pages 6-30, the literature compilation of insect body masses and metabolic rates, 391 records, each row citing its source as a superscript number on the species name or as an asterisk for the compilers' unpublished measurements; the numbered reference list 1-115, pages 31-36). The same supplement was downloaded by M. Novak as the Word file `fec1245_supmat.doc` on 2026-09-28 and tracked here until 2026-10-04, when the owner replaced it by the PDF (issue #63); the first parse (`textutil -convert txt` of the .doc, a Python parser anchored on the Method and Wing-status columns, EndNote field codes stripped) gave the CSVs the database carried from 2026-09-28 to that date. Parsed with `parse_chown_supmat.py` (PyMuPDF; the superscripts are told apart from the species text by their font size and superscript flag, table rows by the cells' baselines and columns by the cell edges of the table's spacer rows) into `AppendixS1_ant_mass_parsed.csv` (244 records, 8 species; identical to the first parse), `AppendixS2_insect_mass_parsed.csv` (391 records, 382 distinct species strings; columns as before plus `ref_keys`) and `references.csv` (`key`, `raw_citation`: the 115 numbered entries verbatim apart from the joining of wrapped lines and whitespace normalisation, plus key `*` with the footnote text "Chown lab, unpublished data." and key `S1` with the heading and footnote of Appendix S1). Checks built into the parser: the reference numbers run 1-115 without gaps or duplicates, all 115 are cited by at least one S2 row, the first citations appear in numerical order, and every cited number has an entry. Re-create the three files with `python3 parse_chown_supmat.py [path to the PDF]` (needs `pymupdf`).
Licence: publisher file (README; https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2435.2007.01245.x). `fec1245_supmat.pdf` is the Wiley Supporting Information file, tracked since 2026-10-04 in place of the .doc it replaced (licence audit, issue #6): copyright with the publisher, kept for reproducibility of the parser, not covered by the repository licence. The two parsed csv files are our transcriptions (facts); see sources/LICENSES.md.

Columns used: Species, Mass (mg), Family, Order; `ref_keys`.
Record set: all 391 S2 rows and the 244 S1 rows. The first parse recovered only the 347 S2 rows that have both a Method and a Wing-status cell; the 44 rows with a blank Method (15) or Wing-status (29) cell (all with a mass, a metabolic rate and a reference mark) were found when the PDF was parsed and added on the owner's decision of 2026-10-04 (issue #63): 36 of them are identified to species and enter the database (`BodyMass_Chown_etal_2007.r` checks `nrow(s2) == 391`), the eight `sp.` rows fall out as before. Values of the 347 rows are identical to the first parse (the metabolic-rate cells printed with a space inside the number, '5686. 9', are now '5686.9'; `Karoophasma biedouwensis` has its order 'Mantophasmatodea' typed into the family cell of the supplement, split by the parser as before).
Filters: unidentified taxa ('Species 1', 'sp.', 'nr.') dropped; subspecies truncated.
Mass type: live body mass (mg -> g), as used in the paper's metabolic-scaling analysis; no conversion.
Imputed rows: none flagged in source (measured masses of the metabolic-rate specimens).
Provenance: compilation (registry `Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`: class `compilation`, default type `unknown`). Hop 1 through the Appendix S2 superscript numbers kept as `ref_keys` (`SplitRefKeys(s2$ref_keys, ';')`; one mark per row, 115 numbers and `*`), resolved in `references.csv` -> `primary_references.csv` (`R/library/citations/`, `reflist_specs$Chown_etal_2007`; the Nature reference style of the list is parsed by `ParseNatureStyle()`). All 581 kept records are keyed (115 distinct keys): the 337 S2 records cite 113 numbered references or `*` (Chown lab unpublished, role `self`), and the 244 Appendix S1 ant records carry the key `S1` (the appendix heading and footnote; the ants were measured by the authors, role `self` by owner decision of 2026-10-04), so both resolve to the compilation itself (`measured_in_source`).
