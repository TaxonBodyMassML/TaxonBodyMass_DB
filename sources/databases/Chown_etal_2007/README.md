# Chown et al. (2007) insect metabolic rate compilation

Source: Chown, S. L., Marais, E., Terblanche, J. S., Klok, C. J., Lighton, J. R. B., & Blackburn, T. M. (2007) Scaling of insect metabolic rate is inconsistent with the nutrient supply network model. Functional Ecology 21:282-290. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2435.2007.01245.x
Data: Wiley Supporting Information `fec1245_supmat.pdf` (36 pages; the publisher's Supporting Information file for the article: Appendix S1, pages 1-5, metabolic rate data for eight size-polymorphic ant species, 244 individual records; Appendix S2, pages 6-30, the literature compilation of insect body masses and metabolic rates, 391 records, each row citing its source as a superscript number on the species name or as an asterisk for the compilers' unpublished measurements; the numbered reference list 1-115, pages 31-36). The same supplement was downloaded by M. Novak as the Word file `fec1245_supmat.doc` on 2026-09-28 and tracked here until 2026-10-04, when the owner replaced it by the PDF (issue #63); the first parse (`textutil -convert txt` of the .doc, a Python parser anchored on the Method and Wing-status columns, EndNote field codes stripped) gave the CSVs the database carried from 2026-09-28 to that date. Parsed with `parse_chown_supmat.py` (PyMuPDF; the superscripts are told apart from the species text by their font size and superscript flag, table rows by the cells' baselines and columns by the cell edges of the table's spacer rows) into `AppendixS1_ant_mass_parsed.csv` (244 records, 8 species; identical to the first parse), `AppendixS2_insect_mass_parsed.csv` (391 records, 382 distinct species strings; columns as before plus `ref_keys`) and `references.csv` (`key`, `raw_citation`: the 115 numbered entries verbatim apart from the joining of wrapped lines and whitespace normalisation, plus key `*` with the footnote text "Chown lab, unpublished data."). Checks built into the parser: the reference numbers run 1-115 without gaps or duplicates, all 115 are cited by at least one S2 row, the first citations appear in numerical order, and every cited number has an entry. Re-create the three files with `python3 parse_chown_supmat.py [path to the PDF]` (needs `pymupdf`).
Licence: publisher supplementary file (Wiley); the PDF has been tracked in this folder since 2026-10-04 in place of the .doc it replaced (licence audit: issue #6).

Columns used: Species, Mass (mg), Family, Order; `ref_keys`.
Record set: the first parse recovered the 347 S2 rows that have both a Method and a Wing-status cell; the 44 rows with a blank Method (15) or Wing-status (29) cell (all with a mass, a metabolic rate and a reference mark; 36 of them identified to species) were found when the PDF was parsed and are held back by `BodyMass_Chown_etal_2007.r` (`stopifnot(nrow(s2) == 347)`) until the owner decides whether to add them (issue #63). Values of the 347 rows are identical to the first parse (the metabolic-rate cells printed with a space inside the number, '5686. 9', are now '5686.9'; `Karoophasma biedouwensis` has its order 'Mantophasmatodea' typed into the family cell of the supplement, split by the parser as before).
Filters: unidentified taxa ('Species 1', 'sp.', 'nr.') dropped; subspecies truncated.
Mass type: live body mass (mg -> g), as used in the paper's metabolic-scaling analysis; no conversion.
Imputed rows: none flagged in source (measured masses of the metabolic-rate specimens).
Provenance: compilation (registry `Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`: class `compilation`, default type `unknown`). Hop 1 through the Appendix S2 superscript numbers kept as `ref_keys` (`SplitRefKeys(s2$ref_keys, ';')`; one mark per row, 115 numbers and `*`), resolved in `references.csv` -> `primary_references.csv` (`R/library/citations/`, `reflist_specs$Chown_etal_2007`). Of the 545 kept records, the 301 S2 records are keyed (111 distinct keys; `*` = Chown lab unpublished, role `self`) and the 244 Appendix S1 ant records carry no reference mark (`ref_keys` NA; the supplement gives no source for S1). Downstream, Ehnes_etal_2011 and Makarieva_2008 (Table S3) copy this compilation and Herberstein_etal_2022 cites it for the eight S1 ant species (`Bib/source_dependencies.csv`), so their rows resolve through these references at hop 2. Verification round: see `audit/provenance_rounds.md`.
