# Fisher, Owens & Johnson (2001) marsupial life-history compilation body masses

Source: Fisher, D. O., Owens, I. P. F., & Johnson, C. N. (2001) The ecological basis of life history variation in marsupials. Ecology 82:3531-3540. https://doi.org/10.1890/0012-9658(2001)082[3531:TEBOLH]2.0.CO;2
Data: `appendixA.csv`, our transcription of the published Appendix A, and `appendixA_references.bib`, its reference list.
Licence: transcription (facts) (README; https://doi.org/10.1890/0012-9658(2001)082[3531:TEBOLH]2.0.CO;2). `appendixA.csv` and `appendixA_references.bib` are our transcription of the published Appendix A and are attributed to the article; the article is not stored.
Source label: `Fisher_2001`.

Columns used, Filters, Mass type and Imputed rows: this minimal README was written for the licence audit (issue #6, 2026-10-06); the full README in the convention of the other source folders is pending. The parse script `BodyMass_Fisher_2001.r` documents the columns read and the filters applied.
