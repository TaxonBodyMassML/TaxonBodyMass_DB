# Lemoine et al. (2026) soil macrofauna of Martinique: individual fresh masses of the image-allometry calibration set

Source: Lemoine, M., Lechopier, T., Marichal, R., Molin, N., & Coulis, M. (2026) Estimating the biomass of soil macrofauna communities using composite images. European Journal of Soil Biology 130:103854. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejsobi.2026.103854 (open access, CC BY 4.0; Crossref: issued 2026-09, deposited 2026-08-06; green copies on HAL hal-05725135 and Agritrop 618948). `Citation.bib` is the publisher's export (key `LEMOINE2026103854`); the curated entry in `Bib/TaxonBodyMass_Citations.bib` is `Lemoine:2026aa` (drafted from that export; the owner adds the `BM_citations` Sheet row, issue #76).
Data: the calibration data set of the paper, deposited as "An individual-level dataset combining fresh and dry biomass of soil macrofauna associated with 2D image-derived measurements" (Coulis, M.; CIRAD Dataverse, https://doi.org/10.18167/DVN1/SCH4AV, version 1.0 released 2026-08-03, CC BY 4.0): `mass_surface_data.csv` (335,919 bytes, md5 `d5670a45def9b33b8502219c0d7a4c7f`; semicolon-separated, decimal commas, CRLF, UTF-8) and `mass_surface_labels.csv` (8,030 bytes, md5 `69a64f9c05ae8248058b26627332849a`; latin1), the column dictionary. Both files were downloaded from the Dataverse API on 2026-10-04 (`?format=original`) and replace the comma-separated copies the owner had placed in this folder: those copies were a re-export in which 36 fresh-mass cells with five decimals had been read as 10x or 100x the deposited value (`1,35975` -> `135,975`; all 16 `Cloporte Blanc` rows, 11 *Cubaris murina*, 7 *Glomeridesmus marmoreus*, 2 *Spherarmadillo* cf. *nebulosus*) and one cell was unparseable (`1,503,665` for the deposited `15,03665`). The file has 1,163 rows (the paper counts 1,153 individuals; 1,151 rows carry a fresh mass, 921 a dry mass), one per individual: `mass_fresh_mg`, `mass_dry_mg`, `species` ("species name (or a code for morphotype)"), `class`, `order`, `family`, `larve` (TRUE for a larval stage, FALSE otherwise, NA on 186 rows), `id_morphotype` (individual id) and 34 ImageJ measurements of the scanned individual (`Area`, `Perim`, ..., `Solidity`), which are not used. Soil macroinvertebrates (> 2 mm) of 7 classes, 25 orders, 58 families and 96 species or morphospecies were collected alive by visual searching and Barber traps in agroecosystems and natural forests of Martinique between February 2021 and October 2023, identified to family with a general key and to species by experts of each group, and weighed within 24 h of collection on a Sartorius ME5 (±0.001 mg) or, above 5.1 g, a Mettler Toledo AG104 (±0.1 mg) microbalance after cleaning (earthworms rinsed and blotted); dry mass was measured after freezing at -70 °C and freeze-drying, on the individuals for which this was possible (Methods 2.1-2.2 and Table 1 of the paper). Within species the authors sampled "the entire range of body sizes, from the smallest juvenile stages to fully grown adults" (Methods 2.1).
Licence: CC BY 4.0 (README: the CIRAD Dataverse record; https://doi.org/10.18167/DVN1/SCH4AV). The two csv files are tracked.

Columns used: `species` (the raw name), `mass_fresh_mg`, `larve`, `class`, `order`, `family` (taxonomy hints; the phylum is derived from the class). `mass_dry_mg`, `id_morphotype` and the image measurements are not used.
Filters: rows without a fresh mass are dropped (12 individuals with a dry mass only: five *Chondromorpha xanthotricha*, five *Nanostreptus geayi*, one *Dichogaster bolaui*, one *Haplocyclodesmus angustipes*; each of these species keeps weighed fresh individuals, so the dry masses are not converted). Of the 96 labels of `species`, only plain binomials are kept (56 labels); the label `Curculionidae (Metamasius hemipterus)`, the family with the identification in brackets, is read as *Metamasius hemipterus*, and `Clivina (Paraclivina) tuberculata` keeps its subgenus for the subgenus rule of `FixFormatting()`, which folds it with the source's `Clivina tuberculata`. Everything else leaves through `DropImputed()` (see Imputed rows): two identifications qualified by cf. and 36 morphospecies codes or placeholders, the genus- and family-level ones (`Nylanderia sp`, `Newportia sp`, `Solenophorus sp`, `Amphiacusta sp`, `Ataenius_sp`, `Cariblatta_sp`, `Blattodea_sp2`, `Carabidae_sp1 (Selenophorus sp)`, `Coccinellidae_sp`, `Gryllidae sp1`, `Hygrophilidae_sp`, `Rhinodrilidae_sp`, `Phyllophaga sp - Larve`, `larve sp1`) and the genus-less codes that the raw-name vocabulary of `audit/raw_name_patterns.csv` cannot read (`sp_isoptera`, `sp_Lepidoptera`, `sp1_formicidae`, `Sp_Staphylinidae`, `Larve_coleo`, `Cloporte Blanc` (French: white woodlouse), `Orthoporus R.lézarde` (a genus with a locality code), ...). The parser drops them all before the cleaning chain, so none of this source's names reaches `FixFormatting()` as a placeholder. Individuals flagged `larve = TRUE` are dropped (58 rows of 9 kept species; holometabolous larvae and the nymphs of cockroaches, earwigs and ants; *Hermetia illucens* occurs only as larvae and leaves with them); rows with `larve` NA (not recorded) are kept, since an empty flag is not evidence of a life stage, and the `larve = FALSE` rows include juveniles of all sizes by the authors' design (Data). 800 rows kept.
Mass type: fresh (live) body mass in mg of the weighed individual, converted to grams (/ 1000); no other conversion. The dry masses of the file and the fresh- and dry-mass estimates of the paper's area/perimeter regressions are not used, so no value is image-derived or modelled. One row per individual (`n = 1`), 1-34 individuals per species, so the species value is the geometric mean over field individuals of all post-larval sizes (Pass 1 of RunMe.r), not an adult mean.
Imputed rows: none flagged in source. Dropped through `DropImputed()` and logged to `audit/imputed_rows.csv`, in this order: 22 rows (2 labels) whose identification is qualified by cf. (*Olios* cf. *sanctivincenti*, *Spherarmadillo* cf. *nebulosus*); 271 rows (36 labels) of morphospecies codes and genus- or family-level placeholders (Filters); 58 rows (9 species) flagged as larval or nymphal (`larve = TRUE`). 800 rows kept.
Source label: `Lemoine_2026`.
Record set: 800 records of 56 raw labels; `Clivina (Paraclivina) tuberculata` folds with `Clivina tuberculata` and the misspelt *Chodromorpha* is corrected, so 55 cleaned names enter the enrichment, all resolved by GBIF (confidence 95-99): 55 rows in `TaxonBodyMass.csv`, 52 of them species new to the database (first run, 2026-10-04) and three shared with `Herberstein_etal_2022` (*Ectatomma ruidum*, *Periplaneta americana*, *Wasmannia auropunctata*); 41 genera new to `TaxonBodyMass_GenusLevel.csv`. GBIF files eight names under another accepted name (`species_changed`): *Acanthoscurria antillensis* -> *A. maga*, *Cyclocephala annamariae* -> *C. tridentata*, *Dysdercus delauneyi* -> *D. fulvoniger*, *Eidmanella pallida* -> *Eidmannella pallida*, *Tomarus ebenus* -> *Euligyrus ebenus*, *Polypheretima elongata* -> *Perichaeta elongata*, *Mycetomoellerius jamaicensis* -> *Trachymyrmex jamaicensis*, *Lepisma saccharina* -> *L. saccharinum*; *Trigoniulus coralinus* matches *T. corallinus* (fuzzy, 95; `reports/warnings_name_change.md`). No name is unresolved and no value is flagged in `reports/warnings_mass_values.md`. The species values are field geometric means over 1-34 individuals of all post-larval sizes (Mass type), e.g. the tarantula *Acanthoscurria antillensis* 0.41 g over 12 individuals of 19-2,510 mg.
Provenance: primary (`Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`: class `primary`, default `measured_in_source`, tier F). Every value is the authors' own weighing of a field individual (Methods 2.2); the data set cites no other study for a mass, the only entry of `Citation.bib` is the paper itself, and the records carry no `ref_keys`, so the source is attributed at hop 0 to itself like the other primary sources and has no `primary_references.csv` and no `reflist_specs` entry. The paper's own reference list (49 entries, length-mass allometries and preservation studies) concerns the method, not the values. No source in the database copies these values (the data set was released in August 2026), so `Bib/source_dependencies.csv` has no edge for this label. Spelling: the source writes *Chodromorpha xanthotricha* on the four fresh-weighed individuals and *Chondromorpha xanthotricha* on the five dry-only ones, and *Scolopocryptos ferrugineus* for *Scolopocryptops ferrugineus* (family Scolopocryptopidae in the same row); both are corrected in `R/library/fix_misspellings.r`.
