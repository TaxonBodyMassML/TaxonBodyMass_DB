# Makarieva et al. (2008) metabolic-rate compilation body masses

Source: Makarieva, A. M., Gorshkov, V. G., Li, B.-L., Chown, S. L., Reich, P. B., & Gavrilov, V. M. (2008) Mean mass-specific metabolic rates are strikingly similar across life's major domains: Evidence for life's metabolic optimum. PNAS 105:16994-16999. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0802148105
Data: ten csv files (`S1a`, `S2b`, `S3`, `S4`, `S5a`-`S5c`, `S6a`-`S6b`, `S7`), our transcriptions of the SI tables of the article.
Licence: transcription (facts) (README; https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0802148105). The ten csv files are our transcriptions of the SI tables of the PNAS article and are attributed to it with the notice PNAS asks for, "Copyright (2008) National Academy of Sciences"; the article and its SI are not stored.
Source label: `Makarieva_2008`.

Columns used, Filters, Mass type and Imputed rows: this minimal README was written for the licence audit (issue #6, 2026-10-06); the full README in the convention of the other source folders is pending. The parse script `BodyMass_Makarieva_etal_2008.r` documents the columns read and the filters applied.
