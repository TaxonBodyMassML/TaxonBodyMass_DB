# Mean mass-specific metabolic rates across life's major domains (Makarieva et al. 2008)

Source: Makarieva, A. M., Gorshkov, V. G., Li, B.-L., Chown, S. L., Reich, P. B. & Gavrilov, V. M. (2008) Mean mass-specific metabolic rates are strikingly similar across life's major domains: Evidence for life's metabolic optimum. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 105:16994-16999. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0802148105. Bib key `Makarieva:2008aa` (`Citation.bib`).
Data: the Supporting Information PDF (`pnas08SI.pdf`, 212 pages; the publisher's file, tracked since 2026-10-06 (owner rule, issue #124), copyright (2008) National Academy of Sciences; also at the authors' mirror http://www.bioticregulation.ru/common/pdf/pnas08/pnas08SI.pdf). Its Datasets S1-S11 give, per taxonomic group, one table of metabolic rates with body masses and the group's own reference list. The committed `S*.csv` files are the 2026-08 transcriptions of the tables ingested here (columns as printed plus `mass_g`): `S1a.csv` heterotrophic prokaryotes (245 rows; cell mass estimated by the authors from linear dimensions), `S2b.csv` heterotrophic protozoa (52 rows, the per-species minima of Table S2a), `S3.csv` insects (402 rows; Chown et al. 2007 Appendix S2 plus ten rows the authors added, printed in green in the SI), `S4.csv` aquatic invertebrates (809 rows), `S5a.csv` amphibians (158) and `S5b.csv` reptiles (156) after White et al. (2006), `S5c.csv` fishes (266; FishBase minima), `S6a.csv` birds (321; McKechnie & Wolf 2004 pooled with V. Gavrilov's measurements), `S6b.csv` Gavrilov's bird measurements (207 rows), `S7.csv` cyanobacteria (75). Datasets S8-S11 (microalgae, macroalgae, leaves, plant seedlings) are not ingested (autotrophs).

Columns used: the species (or valid name) and `mass_g`; the family and order of S3 and S7 as the taxonomy seed; since Stage 2 of issue #1 the reference keys of `record_refs.csv` (below).
Filters: rows without a numeric `mass_g` and the placeholders `Unknown_genus`, `Unknown_species` and `Unidentified` are dropped (2,691 table rows -> 2,610 records). The pipeline's name resolution removes the unidentified taxa of S3 ('Species 1 (Sutherland)*', 'Unknown genus', '... sp.') and the strain-level prokaryote names it cannot place.
Mass type: wet body mass in grams as printed (`Mg`, `M (mg)` / 1000, or the cell mass `Mpg` x 1e-12); S1a, S2b and S7 cell masses are the authors' estimates from linear dimensions or from the cited size source, S5c masses are FishBase's, S6a masses are the mean masses of the compiled birds.
Imputed rows: none.
Licence: publisher file (README; https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0802148105). `pnas08SI.pdf` is the PNAS Supporting Information file, tracked since 2026-10-06 (owner rule of issue #124: all source files including publisher supplements are committed; before, it was fetched from the authors' mirror and kept untracked): copyright (2008) National Academy of Sciences, kept for reproducibility of `parse_makarieva_si.py`, not covered by the repository licence. The ten `S*.csv` tables and `S2a_parsed.csv`, `S3_parsed.csv`, `S6b_parsed.csv` are our transcriptions of the SI tables (facts), attributed with the notice PNAS asks for, "Copyright (2008) National Academy of Sciences"; `references.csv`, `record_refs.csv` and `primary_references.csv` are our working tables (repository licence, as for the other sources).
Source label: `Makarieva_2008`.

## Record set

2,610 records, one per table row (`n = 1`): S1a 178, S2b 52, S3 402, S4 808, S5a + S5b 314, S5c 266, S6a 321, S6b 207, S7 62. Known transcription faults of the committed tables, found when the SI was re-read for the reference keys (2026-10-06; the record set is left as it is, the owner's call): 18 rows of `S4.csv` were mis-split, so their `Mg` holds a neighbouring column (rows 13, 25, 45, 111, 130-135, 419, 567-568, 584-586, 784, 790: 'Arietellus cf.', 'Bathycalanus sp.', 'Megacalanus sp.', 'Gaussia princeps', 'Idotea baltica', 'Cleistosoma edwardsii' (species cell 'edwardsii MIN'), 'Gennadas kempi', 'Crossota sp.', 'Pantachogon sp.'; the PDF values are in `record_refs.csv`'s log line of `parse_makarieva_si.py`); `S6b.csv` lacks 11 of the 218 rows of Table S6b (Parus ater, Taeniopygia castanotis, Phoenicurus phoenicurus, Passer domesticus bactrianus x2, Chloris chloris, Turdus merula, Corvus corone cornix x4) and truncates the multi-line reference cells; `S1a.csv` row 245 ('Yersinia pestis') carries the rest of the page in its `valid_name`; `S3.csv` drops the trailing numeral of 'Species 2', 'Griburius sp. 1', 'Unknown genus 1' and the like; `S2b.csv`'s Podophrya fixa prints qWkg 2.9 where Table S2a has 0.52.

## Provenance

Provenance: compilation (`Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`: class `compilation`, default type `unknown`). Every record carries `ref_keys` (`SplitRefKeys()`, 2,610 of 2,610; 449 records cite two or more keys) joined by table row from `record_refs.csv`, which `parse_makarieva_si.py` writes from the SI PDF (PyMuPDF; the landscape pages are rotated, so a cell's start is its `y1`; the S1a-S4-S7 Source cells are justified and wrap, the S3 marks are 6.5 pt superscripts, S6b reference cells wrap below their row). The script also writes `references.csv` (`key`, `citation` verbatim, `note`, `owner_review`: 619 entries -- the lists of Tables S1a (227), S2a (124), S3 (the numbered list 1-115, byte-identical to Chown et al. 2007's Appendix S2 list), S4 (88), S6b (21) and S7 (40), plus the four works the dataset notes name for whole tables) and three parsed tables (`S2a_parsed.csv`, `S3_parsed.csv` with the superscripts and the green flag, `S6b_parsed.csv` with the full reference cells). Keys are `<table>:<author-year>` built from each entry's author block (`S1a:Jurtshuk & McQuitty 1976`, `S6b:Gavrilov 1980a`); the S3 numbers are `S3:1`-`S3:115`. How each table is keyed:

- S1a and S7: the `Source` column (the respiration study) and, in its brackets, the cell-size source where it is a citation or Bergey's Manual (`BM` -> `S1a:Holt 1984`, `BM9` -> `S1a:Holt et al. 1994`, role `compilation`); a culture-collection image or a shape in the bracket gives no key. Both keys are kept because the database value is the mass, which the size source gives, while the SI's own convention names the respiration study.
- S2b: the Table S2a row with the species' minimum qWkg and its Mpg, whose `Reference` column names the study (the `Source` number is the entry of the Vladimirova & Zotin 1983 data base, or OTHER); two rows share the minimum of Astasia klebsii (both keyed).
- S3: the 391 black rows are Chown et al. (2007) Appendix S2 (388 match a `Chown_etal_2007` record by species and mass and their superscripts agree with Chown's marks on every one; 3 differ in the printed name) and are keyed `S3:Chown et al. 2007` (role `compilation`), so that they route through `Chown_etal_2007` at hop 2 (the curated `Chown:2007aa` carries the DOI); the superscripts are kept in `source_text`. The 11 green rows (the authors' additions; the SI says ten) are keyed to their numbered reference (`S3:16`, `S3:20`, `S3:39`, `S3:56`, `S3:101`).
- S4: the `Source` column, plus the 'data of X' study where the cell names one and the S4 list holds it ('Byzova 1973 (data of Müller 1943)' keeps Byzova only; 'Wieser 1963, 1965' gives two keys); `S4:Seibel 2007` (38 cephalopod rows, the per-species minima of Seibel's data base) is role `compilation`.
- S5a and S5b: `S5:White et al. 2006` (role `compilation`; White et al.'s own ESM list is a possible later hop). S5c: `S5c:FishBase` (role `database`, `owner_review`: no DOI).
- S6a: the MW rows `S6:McKechnie & Wolf 2004` (role `compilation`; Table A1 of that paper is the Reynolds & Lee 1996 compilation); the G rows the references of the Table S6b row of that species with the minimum night-time rate (the SI's rule), which reproduces the S6a value on every one of the 110 rows. S6b: the `References` column, one key per Gavrilov paper ('Gavrilov, 1980abc, 1982ab' -> five keys; 'Gavrilov, Dolnik, 1985' -> `S6b:Gavrilov & Dolnik 1985`). The Gavrilov papers are a coauthor's own published measurements; they are keyed as `measurement`, not `self` (owner's call, see the round log).

Resolution of the record keys against the lists: 21 keys needed a documented fallback (a year letter the list lacks taken as the entry's position among the same-year entries -- `S2a:Edwards & Lloyd 1977 [2]`, `Marshall 1948 [1]`, `Zeledon 1960 [1]`, `[2]`, flagged `owner_review`; the same authors with the year off by one or missing -- Reed & Dugan 1978/1979, Segal & Bloch 1955/1956, Hughes 1966/1965, Eisenberg et al. 1973/1974, Blizzard & Peterson 1962/1963, Edney & Spencer 1956/1955, Cartwright & Cain 1959 against the undated entry, Silverthorn; an abbreviated author list -- Kahn 1981, Loh et al. 1984, Wilson P. 1961, Broady 1991; the only lettered entry of the year -- Gavrilov 1974; a near-identical spelling -- Gerhard/Gerhardt 1950, Bonach/Bohach 1983, Noulihan/Houlihan 1950, Reichle/Reiche 1968). 19 keys cited by kept records are in no SI list and stay unmatched (35 records: S1a Allen et al. 1991, Bisset & Hale 1958, Eichler & Pfennig 1988, Kowalski et al. 1991, Luscombe & Gray 1974, Mickelson 1961, Schäfer 1996 (size sources, one record each); S4 Ellenby 1951, Foulds & Roff 1976, Ikeda & Skjoldal 1982 (2; the list has only 1989, another paper), Robertson et al. 2003 (list 2001), Wieser 1963 1965; S6b Gavrilov 1986 (2), Gavrilov et al. 1995a (2), 1995b; S7 Coleman & Colman 1980 (3), Ortega-Calvo & Stal 1994 (7), Pelroy & Bassham 1973, Roenneberg & Carpenter 1993 (4)). 298 entries of the lists are cited by no kept record (the S3 numbered list but the five green keys, the notes' references, and sources of rows the filters drop).
