# EltonTraits 1.0 (Wilman et al. 2014) bird and mammal body masses

Source: Wilman, H., Belmaker, J., Simpson, J., de la Rosa, C., Rivadeneira, M. M., & Jetz, W. (2014) EltonTraits 1.0: Species-level foraging attributes of the world's birds and mammals. Ecology 95:2027. https://doi.org/10.1890/13-1917.1. Bib key `Wilman:2014aa` (`Citation.bib`).
Data: Ecological Archives E095-178, `metadata.htm` and two class files, both parsed by `BodyMass_Wilman_etal_2014.r` under the one label (the mammal half since #65, owner decision 2026-10-04; birds only before). In the repository since its import from FracFeed_DB (2026-08-20).
- Birds: `BirdFuncDat.txt`, 9,995 rows (9,993 species of the BirdLife V3 / IOC 2.7 taxonomy the metadata describes, plus two rows without a name or mass) with `BirdFuncDatSources.txt` (59 `Ref_` entries; they serve the diet and foraging columns, not body mass, except that its `Ref_1`-`Ref_15` are the Handbook of the Birds of the World volumes the `HBW` source words name).
- Mammals: `MamFuncDat.txt`, 5,403 rows (5,400 species of the Wilson & Reeder 2005 taxonomy, `MSW3_ID`, plus three rows without a name or mass) with `MamFuncDatSources.txt` (176 `Ref_` entries, `Ref_1`-`Ref_178` without `Ref_133` and `Ref_162`; one tab-separated entry per `Ref_ID`, nine of them printed over several lines; Windows-1252 encoded with non-breaking spaces).

Columns used: `Scientific`, `BodyMass-Value` (g), `BodyMass-Source`, `BodyMass-SpecLevel`; birds also `BodyMass-Comment`, `IOCOrder`, `BLFamilyLatin`; mammals also `MSWFamilyLatin` (the file carries no order). `BodyMass-Source` is kept as `ref_keys` (Provenance below).
Filters: rows without a numeric `BodyMass-Value` are dropped (the two bird and three mammal rows without a name). Birds: rows with `BodyMass-Source` 'GenAvg' (genus or family typical value, `BodyMass-SpecLevel` 0) and rows whose `BodyMass-Comment` begins 'Copied' (value copied from another species) are dropped, and so are the 484 'PrimScale' rows, EltonTraits' own estimates scaled from a length through a family-level mass-length relationship fitted to congeners (owner decision 2026-10-04, #65; kept as allometry-derived values before); the 273 records whose `Record-Comment` is 'DataFromSplit' (record created by a taxonomic split; 262 of them carry species-level masses) are kept, their treatment deferred to issue #5 together with the COMBINE split-inherited values. Mammals: rows with `BodyMass-SpecLevel` 0 (`BodyMass-Source` 'Ref_2, Ref_3', Genus Average and Family Average) and rows with `BodyMass-SpecLevel` 2 (`Ref_178`, phylogenetically imputed values) are dropped; every kept mammal row is `BodyMass-SpecLevel` 1 (species-level data). Extinct species among the kept mammal rows (31 of the `Ref_117` rows are MOM's Late Quaternary and historically extinct species, e.g. *Macrotis leucura*, *Lagorchestes leporides*; 36 records left at the 2026-10-04 run) are removed downstream by `R/library/filter_extinct.r` (`audit/extinct_taxa.csv`).
Mass type: wet mass in grams as the compilation reports it; no conversion, except the two `Ref_165` rows (Fooden 1963: *Lagothrix lugens* 6.0, *L. cana* 6.4), which are kilograms entered as grams (woolly monkeys weigh 5-10 kg and the `Ref_117` row of *L. lagotricha* reads 6,299.99 g) and are multiplied by 1,000 in the parser for that reference only (owner decision 2026-10-04, #65; GBIF folds both names into *L. lagotricha*, whose species value fell to 62 g and left the output through the range filter before the correction). For bird source Dunning08 the value is the geometric mean of the sex-specific averages (metadata); the mammal values are the species means of the works cited. One value per species and half (`n = 1`).
Source label: 'Wilman_etal_2014'.
Imputed rows: birds 1,361 of 9,993 rows dropped (870 GenAvg genus/family averages + 7 copied from another species, then the 484 PrimScale rows as 'PrimScale: value scaled by EltonTraits from congeners (BodyMass-Source PrimScale)'), 8,632 kept; mammals 812 rows dropped as 'Ref_2/Ref_3 Genus Average or Family Average (BodyMass-SpecLevel 0)' and 547 as 'Ref_178 phylogenetically imputed value (BodyMass-SpecLevel 2)' (Jetz & Freckleton, masses inferred for data-deficient species from phylogenetic, spatial and environmental information), 4,041 kept; all logged to audit/imputed_rows.csv through `DropImputed()`. 12,673 rows in the frame. Four kept mammal values that conflict with the other sources are registered in audit/flagged_species.csv (method `source_conflict_2026-10-04`): *Mustela itatsi* 57 g (`Ref_121`, an Animal Diversity Web page on *M. sibirica*; the other sources 300-565 g) and *Sicista armenica*, *S. kazbegica* and *S. kluchorica* at 6.4-7.2 g (`Ref_146`; PHYLACINE agrees, COMBINE's 61.8 g is the odd value); the values are kept (owner decision 2026-10-04).
Provenance: compilation (`Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`, default unknown); record-level, hop 1 through `BodyMass-Source`, kept as `ref_keys` and resolved in `references.csv` (columns `key`, `citation`; 185 rows) for the citation tooling of issue #1 (`reflist_specs$Wilman_etal_2014`). `primary_references.csv` holds the `--init` skeleton (67 keys cited by kept records; verification waits for the OpenAlex key) with the owner's decisions of 2026-10-04 in its `role` and `notes` columns: `Ref_117`, `Ref_58`, `Dunning08` and the `HBW` volumes are role `compilation` (`compilation_terminal`, hop 2 for the two database sources through the registry), the five 'Reference lost' keys (`Ref_118`, `Ref_119`, `Ref_126`, `Ref_134`, `Ref_155`; 20 records) and `Other` take the queue decision `drop` (the records cite the compilation only), `Ref_146` is queued for the owner after verification, and DataCite-only DOIs take `nodoi`.
- Mammals: the comma-separated `Ref_` codes of the row (`SplitRefKeys(..., ',')`; one kept row, *Carollia sowelli*, cites two, 'Ref_176, Ref_177'). The 176 `MamFuncDatSources.txt` entries are transcribed verbatim into `references.csv` after decoding from Windows-1252, with non-breaking spaces and the line breaks inside an entry replaced by one space and runs of blanks collapsed; typographic errors are kept ('Univeristy', 'Spitzmause'), `Ref_118` and `Ref_134` read 'Reference lost' (7 and 9 rows) and `Ref_146` is a Cyrillic reference the archive file reduced to question marks ('... 1995 Mockba "Hayka"', 12 rows). The 4,041 kept rows cite 59 keys: `Ref_117` Smith et al. 2003 ('updated version obtained from senior author', 3,415 rows) and `Ref_58` Jones et al. 2009 PanTHERIA (486 rows) are the database's own sources `Smith_2003` and `Jones_2009`, registered as copies in `Bib/source_dependencies.csv` (`Wilman_etal_2014 -> Smith_2003` at 0.001 log10, which takes the rounding of the averaged MOM continent rows and leaves the 153 species whose MOM version differs from v10.2 independent: 74% of the shared species identical, 95% within 0.001, 98% within 0.0105; owner decision 2026-10-04 for the strict tolerance; `Wilman_etal_2014 -> Jones_2009` at 1e-6, 481 of 481 shared species identical); their hop-2 attribution follows the machinery of the Stage 1 pilots. The other 140 rows cite 57 field guides, encyclopaedias, IUCN Red List accounts, Animal Diversity Web pages, journal articles and the two lost references.
- Birds: the `BodyMass-Source` word is the key. 'Dunning08' (8,620 rows) is Dunning (2008) CRC Handbook of Avian Body Masses, a compilation at which the attribution ends (`compilation_terminal`; `Wilman_etal_2014 -> Dunning_2008` in `Bib/source_dependencies.csv`); the EltonTraits files name it only as 'Dunning08' and 'Dunning 2007' (metadata, Costs), so `references.csv` carries the handbook's own citation. 'HBW8', 'HBW10', 'HBW11', 'HBW12', 'HBW13' and 'HBW15' (9 rows) are Handbook of the Birds of the World volumes, cited verbatim from `BirdFuncDatSources.txt` `Ref_8`-`Ref_15`. 'PrimScale' (484 rows, dropped) is EltonTraits' own estimate, 'inferred from select primary sources with mass and length data, and mass-length relationships parameterized at family level' (metadata; `BodyMass-Comment` names the length source on 331 of these rows: 'MarceloR', mobilised or calculated from length estimates by M. Rivadeneira, on 321, a primary work on 10); `references.csv` keeps an entry for the key (the EltonTraits paper) that no kept record cites. 'Other' (3 kept rows, `BodyMass-Comment` 'Subspp', the value of a subspecies; the 7 'Copied from' rows are dropped) resolves to nothing and is queued for the owner.
