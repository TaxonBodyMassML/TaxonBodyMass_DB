# AnimalTraits (Herberstein et al. 2022)

Source: Herberstein, M. E. et al. (2022) AnimalTraits - a curated animal trait database for body mass, metabolic rate and brain size. Scientific Data 9:265. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01364-9
Data: Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.6468938 (CC0), `observations.csv` and `column-documentation.csv`, downloaded 2026-09-27.

Columns used: `species`, `body mass` (standardised, kg; converted to g), `phylum`, `class`, `order`, `family`.
Filters: rows with a body-mass value (2,856 observations, 1,830 species). All sexes retained; RunMe Pass 1 averages within species.
Mass type: wet mass; kg -> g only.
Imputed rows: none flagged in source (AnimalTraits compiles published observations and carries no imputation or inference flag).
Provenance: compilation (registry `Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`: class `compilation`, default type `unknown`). Every observation carries the citation of its originating study in the row (`fullReference`, full text with a DOI on 61% of the body-mass rows; `inTextReference` is the short form); the parser keeps the hash key `h:<sha1-8>` of the normalised `fullReference` as `ref_keys` (`ParseInRowCitations()`, one key per row): 2,856 of 2,856 kept records are keyed, 193 distinct keys (26 further references of the file belong to rows without a body mass and are not verified); the in-text form is kept in the `notes` of `primary_references.csv`. Round of 2026-10-04 (tbmcite 0.1.0): 176 references certain (151 `doi_resolves` from the in-row DOI confirmed at Crossref and OpenAlex, 25 `two_service_agreement`), 6 approved by the owner from the review queue (Seymour et al. 1998 CUP DOI, Boyce et al. 2020 journal version, Bailey et al. 1993, Putnam & Murphy 1982, Krol 1994, Pasquet et al. 2018 -- the last carries a Crossref correction notice, 10.1007/s10164-018-0574-8, recorded in `editorial_notice`), 9 built as DOI-less entries from the stated fields (Warnke 1908; Crile & Quiring 1940, volume and pages supplied by the owner; Mlikovsky 1990 and 2003; Jensen & Nielsen 1975; and four articles whose only DOIs are DataCite deposits or Oxford's Auk DOI unknown to Crossref: MacMillen et al. 1977, Hadley et al. 1981, Chaplin 1982, Anderson 1996), 2 pending (Nielsen 1986, 15 records, and Campbell & Hochachka 2000, 1 record, whose in-row DOIs are truncated in the source file). Records resolved to a primary reference: 2,840 of 2,856 (99.4%), 1,819 of 1,830 input taxa; of the 2,390 record links of the 1,525 accepted species in `TaxonBodyMass_Provenance.csv.gz`, 2,376 (99.4%) carry a primary CiteID (181 distinct). Hop 2: the reference Chown et al. 2007 (244 records, the eight size-polymorphic ant species of its Appendix S1) is itself the source `Chown_etal_2007`; until that source's reference list is ingested its 7 accepted species read `compilation_terminal` citing `Chown_etal_2007` (they will become `compiled_from`, Chown's own measurements, once its S1 keys are `self`); `Chown:2007aa` was given its DOI in the curated bib for this match. Bib entries in `Bib/TaxonBodyMass_PrimaryCitations.bib`, Sheet rows in `BM_primary_citations` (191 appended 2026-10-04). Retraction / correction screen of the accepted and candidate DOIs: Consensus only (Scite's monthly MCP quota exhausted; `Bib/scite_checks.csv`, `checked_by = consensus-mcp`, `notice_type = unchecked`, 179 DOIs confirmed by title; 6 certain DOIs -- Bauchot & Stephan 1966, Pirlot & Stephan 1970, Hails 1983, Baudinette et al. 2000, Nevo & Shkolnik 1974, Krog & Monson 1954 -- and El-Nouty et al. 1978 were not among Consensus's three results and have no screening row); Crossref `update-to` and OpenAlex `is_retracted` consulted for every DOI (one correction, above) -- Scite re-screening is due after 2026-11-01.
