# Traits of lizards of the world (Meiri 2018)

Source: Meiri, S. (2018) Traits of lizards of the world: Variation around a successful evolutionary design. Global Ecology and Biogeography 27:1168-1172. https://doi.org/10.1111/geb.12773. Bib key `Meiri:2018aa` (`Citation.bib`).
Data: the Wiley supplements of the paper. `geb12773-sup-0001-appendixs1.csv` (Appendix S1, the trait table: 6,662 rows, one per species, 50 columns, cp1252), `geb12773-sup-0002-appendixs2.csv` (Appendix S2, the column key) and `geb12773-sup-0003-appendixs3.txt` (Appendix S3, the reference key table: 9,911 rows, columns `paper of` (the author-year key as used in S1), `paper` (the citation) and `Taxa` (the taxa the paper covers); tab-separated, cp1252, quoted multi-line cells).

Columns used: `Binomial` (the name after a 'genus: ' prefix where present), `maximum SVL`, `intercept`, `slope` (the Feldman et al. 2016 equation of the species' clade, column 'mass_equation (Feldman et al. 2016 unless stated)'), `Family`, and the three SVL reference columns (below).
Filters: rows whose mass can be computed (numeric maximum SVL, intercept and slope): 6,633 of 6,662.
Mass type: the body mass is not measured. It is computed here as `10^(intercept + slope * log10(maximum SVL))`, rounded to 3 decimals: the maximum body mass in grams, an allometric estimate (the same quantity Feldman_etal_2016 tabulates; `Bib/source_dependencies.csv` keeps `Meiri_2018 -> Feldman_etal_2016` as `derived_same_input`).
Source label: `Meiri_2018`.

## Record set

6,633 records, one per species (`n = 1`), all binomials; 6,514 species carry the label in `TaxonBodyMass.csv`.

## Provenance

Derived source (`Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`: class `derived`, default type `derived_allometry`, `equation_bibcite` `Feldman:2016aa`). Every record gets an equation row (`ref_role equation`, `Feldman_etal_2016` / `Feldman:2016aa`, hop 1) and, since Stage 2 of issue #1 (2026-10-05), one `ref_role measurement` row of type `derived_allometry` per length source: the parser keeps the three SVL reference columns of Appendix S1 ('References: SVL of unsexed individuals, neonates and hatchlings', 'References: SVL of females', 'References: SVL of males') as `ref_keys`. The fourth reference column, 'References (Biology: all columns except M, N and O)', backs the other traits and not the SVL columns M, N, O, and is not used (one record, *Pholidoscelis turukaeraensis*, has a Biology reference only and stays keyless). The cells hold author-year keys of Appendix S3, comma-separated with a trailing comma (five cells use ';'); the parser joins the three cells with ';' and splits them with `SplitRefKeys(cells, '[,;]')`: 6,632 of 6,633 records keyed, 40,416 record x raw-key pairs, 5,411 distinct raw keys, 1 to 82 keys per record (median 4; the Boulenger catalogues, regional field guides and faunal monographs recur across hundreds of species).

The key table does not key the papers one to one. Of its 9,911 rows, 7 repeat a citation under its key (kept once: 9,904 entries); 869 author-year keys stand for two or more different papers (2,324 entries; 'Boulenger 1887' is four papers, 'Costa et al. 2008' four), and the table carries none of the 'a'/'b' year letters the trait table uses for 117 keys ('Boulenger 1885b', 'Rogner 1997a'). `build_references.r` (run from the repository root) writes two files from Appendices S3 and S1:

- `references.csv` (`key`, `citation`, `note`): one row per entry, the citation verbatim; a key the table holds for several papers becomes `<key> [n]` (n the entry's position among the rows of that key, in the table's order); `note` holds the entry's Taxa text (`taxa: ...`), which is what tells such papers apart.
- `key_map.csv` (`taxon`, `raw_key`, `key`, `method`): how a record's raw key maps to a `references.csv` key where the mapping is not the identity (11,588 pairs; the 28,828 exact unique matches are not written). A key with a year letter is looked up without it (`letter`), a spelling the table does not use by its case-, punctuation- and diacritic-folded form (`normalised`: 'Mcranie et al. 2005', 'de Rooij 1915' -> 'De Rooij 1915', 'Fitzsimons 1943' -> 'FitzSimons 1943'); a key with several entries is resolved to the one entry whose Taxa column names the species (`taxa:species`), else its genus (`taxa:genus`), else its family (`taxa:family`), else the one generic entry that names no family, genus or species (`taxa:generic`); a key that still has several candidates (`ambiguous`) or no entry at all (`not_in_list`) keeps its raw form and is reported as `unmatched_key` in the provenance table. Over the 40,416 pairs: exact 28,828, letter 61, normalised 513, taxa:species 1,440, taxa:genus 1,573, taxa:family 1,224, taxa:generic 911 (34,550 resolved, 85.5%), ambiguous 3,813 (318 raw keys; 'Boulenger 1887' 463, 'Mesquita et al. 2016' 321, 'Boulenger 1885b' 266, 'Dunham et al. 1988' 134, 'Boulenger 1885' 129, 'Grismer 2011' 126, 'Costa et al. 2008' 110, 'Das 2002' 95), not_in_list 2,053 -- before the owner's rules of 2026-10-05 (next paragraph) (533 raw keys: 'Uetz 2006' 155, 'TAU Herpetology Collections' 144 (and 'TAU Herpetology collection' 18, 'TAU Museum'), 'Wilson and Swan 2011' 111, bare years split off a 'Smith 1935, 1981'-style cell (70 '1981', 20 '1942', ...), 'Meiri (own measurements)' 68, 'El Din 2006' 57, personal communications, specimen counts and SVL figures that leaked into the reference cells).
