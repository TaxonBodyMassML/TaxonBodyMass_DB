The parser reads the workbook: the owner's CSV export of the data tab (`CORVIDATA.csv`, same species set and reference strings) carries the body masses as displayed, rounded to one decimal (85.7 for 85.74, 448.8 for 448.76), so it is not used and not committed. One row per species, 135 corvid species in the AviList v2025 taxonomy (22 genera); the compilation measured nothing itself and names the reference of every body mass in `bodymass_ref`.

Columns used (sheet `CORVIDATA`): `scientific_name` (AviList binomial), `bodymass` (species average, g; 'not reported' when missing), `bodymass_ref` (the reference of the value, kept as `ref_keys`). Taxonomy hints are constants (class Aves, order Passeriformes, family Corvidae); `genus` is not read.
Filters: rows without a numeric body mass are dropped (6: Corvus celebensis, C. philippinus, C. pusillus, C. samarensis, C. sierramadrensis with `bodymass_ref` 'not reported', and Pica bottanensis, cited to AVONET but 'not reported'); then the five AVONET copies listed under Imputed rows. 124 of 135 rows are kept. The extinct-in-the-wild Corvus hawaiiensis (IUCN 2024 column) is kept as the other sources of the database keep it (AVONET lists it as extant; `audit/extinct_taxa.csv` does not hold it).
Mass type: wet (live) body mass in grams, species averages copied from the cited references; no conversion. One row per species (`n = 1`).
Imputed rows: none flagged by the source itself. Five AVONET-cited values are not species-specific measurements in AVONET (Supplementary dataset 1, sheet `AVONET1_BirdLife`, checked 2026-10-04): Corvus fuscicapillus (635 g), C. leucognaphalus (401), C. meeki (467) and Cyanolyca pulchra (101.33) carry `Mass.Source` 'Inferred' with 'Body Mass' among `Traits.inferred` (the mass of a related species), Corvus palmarum (289) `Mass.Source` 'EltonTraits_Model'; the Tobias_2022 parser excludes those AVONET rows for the same reason, so their copies are dropped here through `DropImputed()` (logged to `audit/imputed_rows.csv`). AVONET's seven `DataFromSplit` rows (Aphelocoma wollweberi, Cissa jefferyi, Corvus insularis, C. violaceus, Nucifraga hemispila, Pica mauritanica, Platysmurus aterrimus: the value of the parent species before the split) are kept, as the Tobias_2022 parser keeps them pending issue #5.

## Record set

129 of the 135 species carry a body mass (41-1,135 g); `bodymass_ref` cites AVONET (Tobias et al. 2022) on 123 rows (122 with a mass), the Handbook of the Birds of the World vol. 14 (del Hoyo, Elliott & Christie 2009) on 4 (Aphelocoma californica 85.74, Cyanocorax colliei 238, C. formosus 209, C. morio 199), BIRDBASE (Sekercioglu et al. 2025) on 2 (Aphelocoma woodhouseii 65, Pica serica 208.75) and Atwood (1980, North American Bird Bander 4(4):2) on 1 (Aphelocoma insularis 116), and reads 'not reported' on 5. The AVONET values are the two-decimal values of Supplementary dataset 1: 120 of the 122 AVONET-cited masses equal the `AVONET1_BirdLife` row of the same name (AviList Coloeus dauuricus, C. monedula and Corvus torquatus under AVONET's Corvus dauuricus, C. monedula and C. pectoralis) and the other two (Corvus minutus 287.82, Pica nuttalli 158.29) the `AVONET3_BirdTree` row; none differs. Those BirdLife rows are Dunning (87), Updated_literature (21: Birds of the World 15, Dunning 2021 5, Cohn-Haft et al. 2013 1), DataFromSplit (7), Inferred (4, dropped) and EltonTraits_Model (1, dropped). Against the Tobias_2022 frame (BirdLife sheet after that parser's filters) on the raw files: 116 species shared, 113 identical (112 AVONET-cited rows and the HBW-cited Aphelocoma californica, whose 85.74 is AVONET's Dunning value), the three other HBW values differ (238 / 234, 209 / 210, 199 / 204); against Wilman_etal_2014: 92 shared, 86 identical. Pica nuttalli and the two Coloeus are not in the Tobias_2022 frame under these names.
