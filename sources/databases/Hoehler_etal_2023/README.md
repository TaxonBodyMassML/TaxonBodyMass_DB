# Hoehler et al. (2023) metabolic rate of the biosphere, Dataset S01

Source: Hoehler, T. M., Mankel, D., Girguis, P. R., McCollom, T. M., Kiang, N. Y., & Jørgensen, B. B. (2023) The metabolic rate of the biosphere and its components. PNAS 120:e2303764120. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2303764120
Data: PNAS Supporting Information `pnas.2303764120.sd01.xlsx` (sheet Metabolic_Data, 10,530 rows) and SI appendix PDF; downloaded by M. Novak 2026-09-28.

Columns used: `Species`, `tsn rank`, `Group`, `Wet Mass (g)`, Kingdom..Family.
Filters: species/subspecies-rank rows only; autotroph and fungal groups (Seedling, Tree sapling, Eukaryotic Microalgae, Cyanobacteria, Fungi) excluded; the Fishes group excluded (juvenile/small experimental fish, median 0.2-0.7 log10 below other sources); the six rows whose wet mass is a spreadsheet formula from dry mass are dropped; the seven rows whose `Comments` mark the cell size as a genus-level value ('BacDive, GENUS', 'BM Vol 3, genus') are dropped. Subspecies and strain suffixes truncated to binomials.
Mass type: wet mass in grams (entered values); the dry and carbon columns in the file are formulas derived from wet mass and are not used.
Imputed rows: 7 rows with genus-level cell sizes dropped (Comments 'BacDive, GENUS' or 'BM Vol 3, genus'; 4,413 kept), then the 1,497 rows coded 'Hudson et al. (2013)' in `Reference Code` dropped as copies of Hudson_2013 (132 taxon names; 262 of the bird copies carry another species' mass, issue #66); 2,916 kept (both calls logged to audit/imputed_rows.csv).
Copied sources: `Reference Code` names the compilation each row was taken from (21 codes, each resolved to a full citation in the workbook's `Refs` sheet; the `Reference` column holds the same citation in full). The 1,497 rows coded 'Hudson et al. (2013)' are row-by-row copies of Hudson, Isaac & Reuman (2013) Appendix S5, which enters the database directly as `Hudson_2013` with a per-row study key (issue #59): all 604 mammal rows are identical copies, but only 631 of the 893 bird rows are; the other 262 bird masses sit under the wrong species in Dataset S01 (every one of them occurs in Appendix S5 under another species, 43 species affected, exactly 10 rows of every species with more than 10 Hudson rows, e.g. Aptenodytes patagonicus carries the 3.3-4.1 g of Archilochus alexandri). Since issue #66 (2026-10-05) the parser drops all 1,497 Hudson-coded rows through `DropImputed()`: 57 species that had only Hudson-coded rows leave the Hoehler frame (none leaves the database: Hudson_2013 or other sources carry them; Anthobaphes violacea is Hudson's Nectarinia violacea, Poecile cinctus its Poecile cincta, and Hoehler's Haliaeetus leucocephalus and Haematopus moquini copies are the nestling and chick individuals Hudson_2013 drops), and the 75 species that mixed Hudson-coded rows with other codes keep those rows. `Bib/source_dependencies.csv` keeps the edge `Hoehler_etal_2023 -> Hudson_2013` as `provenance_only` (documentation; no Hoehler value copies Hudson any more). The Makarieva et al. (2008), Nagy et al. (1999) and Savage et al. (2004) codes are registered there as copies (issue #5).
Source label: `Hoehler_etal_2023`.
Provenance: compilation (`Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`, default unknown); record-level, hop 1 through `Reference Code`, kept as `ref_keys` (one code per row, no NA) and resolved in `references.csv` (columns `key`, `citation`: the 21 entries of the `Refs` sheet transcribed verbatim, typographic errors included -- 'verebrates', 'Science206', 'Funct. Ecol.18', the Wasylenko entry printed with 2015 in the code and 2014 in the citation; the sheet writes the Tijhuis code 'Tijhuis, et al.
