# Andrassy-formula biomass of predatory soil nematodes, Mononchida (Ghaderi, Nielsen & He 2026)

## Data

Source: Ghaderi, R., Nielsen, U. N., & He, J.-Z. (2026) Transforming morphology into ecology: functional trait-based estimation of biomass and carbon budget in predatory nematodes (Nematoda: Mononchida). Applied Soil Ecology 217:106605. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apsoil.2025.106605 (published online 2025-11-12). Bib key `Ghaderi:2026aa` (`Citation.bib`, BibDesk export by the owner, 2026-10-04).
Data: the article's only supplementary file, `1-s2.0-S0929139325007437-mmc1.xlsx` (Elsevier, 848 kB; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0929139325007437, "Supplementary data"), downloaded by M. Novak 2026-10-04. Fifteen sheets, all with the same 19 named columns (`Journal` = Book/Journal, `Source` = author-year key of the taxonomic description, `Order`, `Suborder`, `Superfamily`, `Family`, `Subfamily`, `Genus`, `Species`, `L` = body length in um, `a` = de Man ratio L / greatest body diameter, `D` = L / a in um, `Life stage` = f, m, J1-J4, `cp group` = 4 throughout, `Fresh W` and `Dry W` in ug, `Pc` = carbon content in ug, `R` = respiration and `Ctot` = daily carbon budget) plus unnamed note columns: `Original` (573 rows, the complete record set, read by the parser), `Juveniles` (14 rows, the J1-J4 rows of `Original`), `G index` (98 female rows with the extra column `G%`, the gonad-to-body-length ratio), and `Order-`, `Suborder-`, `Superfamily-`, `Family-`, `Subfamil(t)y-` and `Genera-males` / `-females` (the adult rows of `Original` split by sex, each with rank-level mean rows whose taxonomy cells are empty). Every value of the other fourteen sheets is a row or an average of rows of `Original`.
Licence: not settled. Crossref lists only Elsevier text-and-data-mining licences for the article, OpenAlex records it as hybrid open access under CC-BY; ScienceDirect refuses non-browser requests, so the licence line of the article page could not be read from this session. The workbook is therefore kept untracked (as the publisher PDFs of Hudson_2013, Cai_etal_2025 and Chown_etal_2007 are) until the owner confirms the CC-BY licence on the article page; the parser needs the file at the path above to run with `recompile = TRUE`.
Taxonomic scope: free-living predatory soil nematodes of the order Mononchida (suborders Bathyodontina and Mononchina; families Anatonchidae, Bathyodontidae, Cobbonchidae, Cryptonchidae, Iotonchidae, Mononchidae, Mononchulidae and Mylonchulidae; 48 genera, 242 binomials, 573 population rows in `Original`). The abstract counts 398 populations, 234 species and 47 genera; the workbook has more rows because a population contributes one row per sex and the juvenile rows are separate. All are extant species described in the cited taxonomic literature.

## Record set

Columns used: `Genus`, `Species` (joined to the binomial), `Fresh W` (the mass), `Life stage` (adult filter), `Source` (kept as `ref_keys`), `Order`, `Family` (taxonomy hints, with `phylum = Nematoda`). `L`, `a` and `D` are read only to check the formula (below). `Dry W`, `Pc`, `R`, `Ctot`, `cp group`, `Journal` and `G%` are not used.
Filters: sheet `Original` only; adults only (`Life stage` f or m; the 14 J1-J4 rows are dropped, see Imputed rows); species-level names only (every row is a clean binomial, so the regex filter removes nothing). One row per population x sex (`n = 1`): 559 records of 242 species (99 species with rows of both sexes, 136 with female rows only, 7 with male rows only; 109 species have one row, 80 two, the rest up to 18), so the species value is the Pass-1 geometric mean of RunMe.r over populations and sexes. Masses range from 0.29 ug (*Caputonchus capitatus* female) to 160 ug (*Paramononchus chingishan* male). The parser stops if a row's `Fresh W` is not the Andrassy value of its own `L` and `a` (relative tolerance 1e-6), so that a weighed value or a changed formula in a later version of the file is noticed rather than taken as derived.
Mass type: fresh (= wet) body mass of one population in ug, converted to grams (x 1e-6); no conversion factor and no conversion CiteID. **The mass is not weighed. It is computed in the source** with the volumetric formula of Andrassy (1956), `Fresh W = L x D^2 / 1.6e6` with `D = L / a`, from the body length and the de Man ratio a that the authors took from the taxonomic description cited in `Source` (holotype/paratype or population means of the describers' specimens); the check in the parser confirms this on all 573 rows with zero residual, and the single note cell of the sheet reads "Based on Andrassy, 1956". `Dry W` is 0.104 x `Fresh W` on every row and `Pc` is `Dry W` / 48, so the dry mass is not an independent measurement either and is not used. The record is an allometry-derived value from measured dimensions of the species itself, which the pipeline keeps by the rule stated in the header of `DropImputed()` (R/library/helpers.r) and the owner decisions of 2026-09-29 on Brocher_etal_2025 and Hishi_etal_2019 (length-derived masses included) and of issue #73 ("documented conversion for dry mass or length-derived values"); Mulder_2011, the database's other soil-nematode source, applies the same Andrassy formula to its own measured individuals. **Owner decision pending (issue #73): keep these estimated masses as a `derived` source, or exclude them under the stricter reading of the scope rule (no estimated or modelled values); nothing weighed exists in the workbook, so there is no measured subset to fall back on.**
Imputed rows: none flagged as imputed, averaged or copied in the sheet `Original` (the rank-level mean rows live in the other sheets, which are not read). The 14 juvenile rows (J1-J4; 5 species, all of which also have adult rows) are dropped through `DropImputed()` as non-adult records (logged to audit/imputed_rows.csv). 559 rows kept. All kept masses are formula-derived species values (see Mass type) and are kept.
Source label: `Ghaderi_2025`.

## Provenance

Provenance: derived (`Bib/source_provenance_classes.csv`, class `derived`, default `derived_allometry`, equation Andrassy 1956 "Die Rauminhalts- und Gewichtsbestimmung der Fadenwurmer (Nematoden)", Acta Zoologica Academiae Scientiarum Hungaricae 2:1-15, not in the curated bib; `equation_bibcite` left empty with a TODO like Brocher_etal_2025 and Hishi_etal_2019, to be verified with role `equation` in a later round). The morphometric inputs L and a of every row come from the taxonomic description cited in the row's `Source` key (author-year as written, 85 distinct keys, 434 journal rows and 139 book rows), kept as `ref_keys`. **The workbook ships no reference list** and the article's Crossref record deposits only the 30 references of the main text (among them Ahmad & Jairajpuri 2010, Andrassy 1956 and Andrassy 2009, but none of the other 82 description papers), so the keys cannot be resolved from anything on disk: they read `uningested` in `TaxonBodyMass_Provenance.csv.gz` until a reference list with titles is available (the article's own text may carry it as an appendix or supplementary references; the owner can read the full text). Key spellings are kept as written, including apparent variants of one work (`Coetze, 1966` / `Coetzee, 1966`; `de Braun & Heyns, 1992` / `de Bruin & Heyns, 1992`; `Mulvey & Jansen, 1967` / `Mulvey & Jensen, 1967`, and `Jansen & Mulvey, 1968`), to be reconciled when the list is ingested. Dependencies: none registered. Mulder_2011 shares four species (*Anatonchus tridentatus*, *Clarkus papillatus*, *Mononchus aquaticus*, *Mononchus truncatus*) with values 0.03-0.19 log10 units apart (independent inputs: Mulder's own Dutch individuals vs. the describers' specimens here); Brose_etal_2018 holds only genus-level Mononchida records (*Clarkus*, *Mononchus*, *Mylonchulus*).
